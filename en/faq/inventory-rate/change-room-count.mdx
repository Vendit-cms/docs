title: "I want to change the number of rooms"
---

<Warning>
  **What does suspending a room do to inventory?**

  - Distribution sells against the number of rooms in operation, so changing a room's status shows up in Distribution immediately.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="[Settings] - [Room types], then pick the room type you want to change.">
    ![Room type step 1](/images/객실타입_6.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press + Add to add a room.">
    ![Room type step 2](/images/객실타입_7.png)

    - To take a room out of service for a while, set its status to suspended.

    ![Room suspended status](/images/객실타입_1\(1\).png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
