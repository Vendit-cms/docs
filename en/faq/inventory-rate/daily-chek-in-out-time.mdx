---
title: "Can check-in and check-out times differ by date?"
description: "Yes. An overnight package's check-in and check-out times can be changed date by date."
---

<Check>
  **Adjusting overnight hours around day use sales, or around cleaning, is done in Distribution. These channels support setting check-in and check-out times by date:**

  - **Yeogiottae Owner App (Motel)**
  - **Yanolja (Motel, Hotel, Guesthouse)**
  - **Coolstay (Motel, Hotel, Pension)**
</Check>
