---
title: "Can Check-in/out differ by date?"
description: "You can change an Overnight package's Check-in/out date by date."
---

<Check>
  **Adjusting overnight hours around day use sales, or around cleaning, is done in Distribution. These channels support setting Check-in/out by date:**

  - **Yeogiottae (Motel)**
  - **Yanolja (Motel, Hotel, Guest house)**
  - **Coolstay (Motel, Hotel, Pension)**
</Check>
