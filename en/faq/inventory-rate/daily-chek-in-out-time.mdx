---
title: "Can check-in and check-out times differ by date?"
description: "Yes, an overnight package's check-in and check-out times can be set date by date."
---

<Check>
  **Adjusting stay times around day use sales, or around cleaning, is done in Distribution. These channels support setting check-in and check-out times by date:**

  - **Yeogi Eottae owner app (motels)**
  - **Yanolja (motels, hotels, guest houses)**
  - **Kkulstay (motels, hotels, pensions)**
</Check>
