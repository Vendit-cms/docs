---
title: "How do timetables work?"
description: "Check-in and check-out times can differ per package on sale, for early check-in, late check-out, and the rest."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press create timetable, then set its name and times.">
    ![Creating a timetable](/images/en-timetable-create.png)

    - Import an existing timetable
    - Pull in the accommodation's default timetable, or one you built earlier.
    - Time increments
      - Check-in and check-out times can be set in 30-minute or 60-minute increments.
      - Where a sales channel does not support 30-minute increments, the time is rounded up.
        - For example,
          - Check-in set to 15:30
          - On a channel that supports 60-minute increments only, it is rounded up to 16:00.

    ![Timetable weekday settings](/images/en-timetable-weekday.png)

    - Check-in and check-out times
      - Set them separately for each day, Sunday through Saturday.
    - Apply to selected days
      - Copy one time and apply it to the days you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it to a package and the timetable applies.">
    ![Connecting a timetable to packages](/images/en-timetable-packages.png)

    - 👉 [Connecting a timetable from the package](https://docs.vcms.io/en/inventory-rate/timetable)
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

<Note>
  **Check-in and check-out times only reach the channels (OTAs) that accept them when inventory and rates sync.**
</Note>

### Channels that `can` change check-in and check-out times

1. Yeogiottae owner app (motels)
2. Coolstay (motels, hotels, pensions)
3. Yanolja (motels, hotels, guest houses)

### Channels that `cannot`

1. Yeogiottae Partner Center
2. Ddnayodotcom
3. Naver
4. Airbnb
5. Onda (hotels, pensions)
6. Agoda
7. Tripdotcom
8. Yanolja (pensions)
