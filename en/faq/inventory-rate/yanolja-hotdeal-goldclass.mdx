---
title: "Can I adjust inventory or rates on Yanolja Hot Deal and Gold Class packages?"
description: "Inventory only. Hot Deal and Gold Class inventory syncs from VCMS like any other package. Rates cannot be adjusted from VCMS."
---

<Note>
  **Why can't rates be adjusted?**

  Hot Deal and Gold Class are Yanolja's own promotional packages, so the rate is fixed at the amount Yanolja sets. It cannot be edited in the Yanolja Partner Center either, and a rate changed in VCMS does not apply. The screens below show it.
</Note>
