---
title: "Can I adjust inventory or rates on Yanolja Hot Deal and Gold Class packages?"
description: "Inventory only. Hot Deal and Gold Class inventory you change in VCMS syncs to the channel like any other package. Rates cannot be adjusted from VCMS."
---

<Note>
  **Why can't rates be adjusted?**

  Hot Deal and Gold Class are Yanolja's own promotional special-price packages, so the rate is fixed at the amount Yanolja sets. It cannot be edited in the Yanolja Partner Center either, and a rate changed in VCMS does not apply. The screens below show it.
</Note>
