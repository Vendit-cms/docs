## How do I check that a closure or rate change reached the channel?

> Check against the Distribution screen in VCMS. If the status is Syncing and you saved, the change is applied to the channel.

VCMS sends the values you save in Distribution to connected channels. Every 5 minutes it checks all dates, and if a channel's value differs, it sets the value back to match VCMS. If you change a value directly in the channel, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes.

1. Check that the sync status in Distribution is Syncing.
   In `Sync stopped` status, changes are not applied to the channel even if you save.
2. If a cell you changed still shows the Temporary change mark, click `Save` at the top.
3. In Settings > Channels > Connected channels > Channel packages, check that the channel package is mapped to a VCMS package and that the inventory and rate sync badges are on.
4. In Settings > Room types, check that Management type is `Managed in CMS`.
   With `Managed in channel`, only bookings sync, and inventory and rates are not applied to the channel.

<Note>
  Not all channels allow rate adjustment. On channels that sell on a rental basis, such as Liveanywhere, Banlife Stay and Camperest, and for properties using a new rate plan on Onda Pension Plus, a rate you change in VCMS is not applied to the channel.
</Note>

<Info>
  Each channel's screen displays information differently. To check Stop sell, use a screen that shows the sell status by date, such as a calendar. Airbnb and Expedia sync every 5 minutes, so a change may not appear right after you save.
</Info>

<Warning>
  If you do not set Occupancy-based pricing on a VCMS package mapped to Agoda, it can stay bookable on Agoda even after you set Stop sell. Be sure to set Occupancy-based pricing on packages mapped to Agoda.
</Warning>

If this does not solve the problem, click the Support button and report it.

## I closed a room type, but some channel packages are still open

> This happens when a lower-level channel package still has a sell setting you changed directly. In the dialog that appears when you set Stop sell, select `Stop sell`.

<Info>
  Distribution is structured in this order: room type, package, channel package. When you change the Sell status of a higher-level item and a lower-level item has settings you changed directly, you can choose to change those settings too or keep them.

  - `Stop sell` or `Sell`: All lower-level items change to the same value.
  - `Keep individual settings`: Lower-level settings you changed directly stay as they are. Channel packages you set to Sell yourself keep selling.
</Info>

1. In Distribution, expand the room type and check for packages or channel packages with a different Sell status.
2. Click the room type's Sell status cell and change it to Stop sell.
3. When the "There are sales items below." dialog appears, select `Stop sell`.
4. Click `Save` at the top to apply the change to the channel.

<Note>
  The same dialog appears when you resume selling. If you select `Keep individual settings`, channel packages you set to Stop sell yourself stay in Stop sell.
</Note>

If this does not solve the problem, click the Support button and report it.

## I want to change the sell status, inventory, and rates for a specific period at once

> With Bulk update in Distribution, you can change the sell status, inventory, and rates for a specific period at the same time.

1. In Distribution, click `Bulk update`.
2. Select the Date and the items under Apply to, then click the Add button at the top.
3. Check the added conditions and click the `Bulk update` button at the bottom.
4. Check the Temporary change items, then click `Save` at the top to apply them to the channel.

<Tip>
  **To set all rooms to Stop sell for a period**

  - In Apply to, select all room types, packages, and channel packages, then change Sell status to Stop sell.
  - To set Stop sell for just a few days, you can also click the Sell status cell in Distribution, change it, and save. Drag left or right to change multiple dates at once.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  - If you leave the screen without clicking `Save`, temporary changes are not applied to the channel.
  - Dates outside the Sync period are not applied to the channel. Manage those dates directly in the channel.
</Warning>

## Can I stop selling on specific days only?

> Yes. In Distribution, you can show only the days of the week you want and set them to Stop sell.

1. In Distribution, open `View settings` and, in Select days of the week, choose only the days to set to Stop sell.
   For example, if you choose only Friday and Saturday, the screen shows only those days.
2. Click the Sell status cell of the date and change it to Stop sell.
3. Click `Save` at the top to apply the change to the channel.
4. When you finish, set Select days of the week in `View settings` back to Every day.

<Info>
  Select days of the week only changes the dates shown on the screen and does not affect sales. If you leave it changed, other dates may look like they have disappeared.
</Info>

## When a booking comes in on one channel, do other channels close automatically?

> Yes. If the room type is Managed in CMS and the status is Syncing, inventory on other channels goes down too.

When a booking comes in on one channel, VCMS reduces the room type's availability and sends the reduced inventory to the other channels connected to the same room type. When availability reaches 0, all mapped channel packages stop selling. If a booking is canceled, inventory goes back up.

<Check>
  **If channels do not close automatically, check the following**

  - In Settings > Room types, check that Management type is `Managed in CMS`. With `Managed in channel`, only bookings sync, and inventory does not sync.
  - Check that the sync status in Distribution is Syncing. In Sync stopped status, only bookings sync.
  - In Settings > Channels > Connected channels > Channel packages, check that all channel packages on sale are mapped to VCMS packages. Unmapped channel packages do not share inventory.
</Check>

<Info>
  On Airbnb, Ddnayodotcom, and Yanolja pension, one channel package sells as one room. When inventory goes down, availability drops to 0 in turn, starting from the bottom channel package in Distribution.
</Info>

<Note>
  - If a guest checks out early, the room does not go back on sale automatically. If you can sell it, open it yourself in Distribution.
  - VCMS cannot detect phone or walk-in bookings. Register them in VCLOUD or reduce the inventory yourself in Distribution.
</Note>
