## VCMS inventory looks different from channel or VCLOUD inventory

> Inventory counts can seem not to match. This is a settings issue, so you can fix it by checking the items below in order.

While Syncing, VCMS checks all dates every 5 minutes. If a channel value differs, VCMS changes it to match the VCMS value. If the numbers keep differing, check the settings below.

1. Check whether Distribution is in `Sync stopped` status.
   In Sync stopped status, only bookings sync. Inventory and rates are not sent to channels.
2. In Settings > Channel > Connected channels > Channel packages, check for unmapped channel packages.
   Bookings that come in through an unmapped channel package do not reduce VCMS inventory. If you sell that package, map it to a VCMS package.
3. In Settings > Room type, check that Management type is CMS.
   With `Managed in channel`, only bookings sync. Inventory and rates do not sync.
4. If you also use VCLOUD, check that both services have the same room types and number of rooms.
   Inventory goes down only when a booking assigned in VCLOUD maps to a VCMS room type.

<Warning>
  **Do not map Yanolja hard block packages**

  A hard block is a package whose rooms the sales channel buys in advance and resells, so you cannot adjust its inventory or rates. Leave it unmapped. Mapping it can affect the count of rooms sold.
</Warning>

<Note>
  - If you change a count directly in a channel, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes while inventory and rate sync is on. Change inventory in VCMS.
  - Setting a room to Stop sell in VCLOUD does not carry over to VCMS. If a guest checks out early, VCMS holds the inventory until the booking's check-out time.
  - Channel screens display information differently from one channel to another. To check for Stop sell, use the calendar screen that shows Sell status by date.
</Note>

If this does not solve the issue, press the Support button and report it. It goes faster if you include the date and room type where inventory differs, the channel package name, the related Booking ID, and a screenshot.

## How do I handle inventory for rooms that are out of order or under construction?

> Remove rooms you cannot use from inventory yourself in VCMS. Setting them to Stop sell in VCLOUD does not change VCMS inventory.

**When the end date is unknown or the period is long**

1. In Settings > Room type, select the room type that has the room.
2. Change the room status of the room that is out of order or under construction to Inactive, then save.
   Inactive rooms leave sellable inventory immediately, and Distribution updates right away.

**When you only need to remove specific dates**

1. In Distribution, lower the availability on those dates by the number of rooms you are removing.
   For a long period, you can change it all at once with `Bulk update`.
2. Press `Save` at the top to apply the change to channels.

<Warning>
  If you set Stop sell only in VCLOUD or on a kiosk, channels keep selling that room. If you also use VCLOUD, handle it in both VCLOUD and VCMS.
</Warning>

<Note>
  When repairs are finished, restore the room status you set to Inactive and the availability you lowered. If you do not, rooms you could sell will not sell on channels.
</Note>

## Inventory shows a negative number. Why?

> Availability such as -1 means more bookings came in for that date than you could sell.

<Info>
  **Common causes**

  - The same room was booked on several channels at the same time, within a few minutes
  - A booking was extended, or another booking came in, on a date where you had manually lowered availability
  - A booking synced from a channel was entered again in VCLOUD or elsewhere, so it was counted twice
</Info>

1. In Bookings, check the bookings that check in on that date and their room types.
2. If the same booking was entered twice, clear up the duplicate you added manually.
   Bookings synced from channels are reflected in inventory without being entered again.
3. If you really are short of rooms, take care of the guest first.
4. To stop taking bookings, set that date to Stop sell in Distribution and press `Save`.

<Warning>
  Do not manually change the availability number to 0. A negative number reflects the actual number of bookings, so changing it can throw off inventory later.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Conversely, a date where you entered more availability than the number of rooms shows `Exceeds availability`. You could sell more rooms than you actually have, so check the count.
</Note>

If this does not solve the issue, press the Support button and report it.

## Are phone and walk-in bookings reflected in channel inventory?

> They reach channels only if you register them in VCLOUD or lower inventory yourself in Distribution.

<Info>
  VCMS has no feature for creating bookings directly. VCMS cannot know about bookings taken outside channels, such as phone or walk-in bookings. Unless you reflect them separately, channels keep selling that room.
</Info>

<Accordion title="When you also use VCLOUD">
  Register it in VCLOUD as a manual booking. Booking info syncs between VCMS and VCLOUD. After registering it, check in Distribution that availability went down on that date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When you use VCMS only">
  1. In Distribution, press the availability cell for the booking date and lower it by the number of rooms booked. If you will not sell any more on that date, you can change Sell status to Stop sell instead.
  2. Press `Save` at the top.
  3. If the booking is canceled, raise the inventory you lowered or remove Stop sell, then press `Save`.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  - If you set Stop sell directly in a channel's Partner Center, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes while inventory and rate sync is on, and the room goes back on sale. Set Stop sell in Distribution.
  - If you enter a booking synced from a channel into VCLOUD again, inventory is deducted twice. Enter only bookings you took outside channels.
</Warning>
