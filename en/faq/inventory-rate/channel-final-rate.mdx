---
title: "I want to mark up or discount one sales channel only"
description: "Set a final channel rate and you can keep rates on channels like Agoda and Trip.com, where promotions run hard, in line with the rest."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="In [Settings] - [Channels] - [Property details], press the final channel rate button for the property you are connecting.">
    <Frame>
      ![Final channel rate step 1](/images/채널_2-3.png "Final channel rate step 1")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the final channel rate and enter the markup or discount weighting.">
    <Frame>
      ![Final channel rate step 2](/images/채널_3-2.png "Final channel rate step 2")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, and the weighting applies to the final channel rate.">
    For example, set a -30% weighting on Yanolja's final channel rate and a package at KRW 100,000 goes to Yanolja at KRW 70,000.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The **final channel rate** lets you send a different final price to each channel when rates leave the Distribution screen. Use it to bring channels like Agoda and Trip.com, where promotions run hard, into line with the others.

  Set a -30% weighting on Yanolja's final channel rate and a package at KRW 100,000 arrives on Yanolja at KRW 70,000.
</Info>

<Tip>
  The final channel rate is set per channel on the channel settings screen. See ["Final channel rate" in the channel connection guide](/en/channels/channel-setting) for the details.
</Tip>
