---
title: "How do I set inventory and rates?"
description: "Set inventory and rates on the VCMS package, then link the channel package to it and those values sync to the channel."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start by creating packages from the room types and rates your property already uses.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the package in VCMS on top of a room type.">
    Create the package from the room types and rates your property uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the sales channel's packages to your VCMS packages so inventory and rate management run cleanly.">
    Channel packages sitting under a linked package all take the same package rate, and they share inventory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the channel package to the VCMS package.">
    A linked channel package takes the VCMS package's rate exactly, and shares its inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If you use the Vendit VCLOUD service, a channel package not linked to a package is created under an unassigned room type.
</Info>
