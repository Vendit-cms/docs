---
title: "Ddnayodotcom multi-night bookings arrive as several bookings"
description: "Book several nights on Ddnayodotcom and VCMS receives that booking split night by night. A 3-night booking becomes 3 bookings, a 15-night booking becomes 15."
---

<Info>
  **Why split by night?**

  Ddnayodotcom sometimes bundles separate bookings into one. That can make the night count come out wrong, so VCMS manages the booking in single nights. This way, inventory stays accurate even when part of a booking changes or is cancelled.
</Info>
