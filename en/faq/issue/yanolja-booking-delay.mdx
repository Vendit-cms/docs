<Check>
  Delayed Yanolja booking sync is caused by a problem on the Yanolja channel.

  - Inventory and rate sync keep working, but Yanolja booking sync can run more than an hour behind.
  - On the front desk computer you use, open the Yanolja booking list and check whether the Yanolja system error below appears. 👉 [Yanolja booking search](https://partner.yanolja.com/reservation/search)
    <img
      src="/images/blocked.png"
      alt="Yanolja system error"
      style={{ width:"89%" }}
    />
</Check>

<Warning>
  Check the Yanolja `pause` setting that guards against overbooking.

  - Without the Yanolja pause setting, you have to manage inventory for Yanolja bookings yourself until booking sync recovers, or you risk overbooking.
    <img
      src="/images/야놀자시스템오류모달-1.png"
      alt="Yanolja system error dialog"
      title="Yanolja system error dialog"
      className="mr-auto"
      style={{ width:"53%" }}
    />
    > ### What is the Yanolja pause setting?
    >
    > - When a Yanolja system error delays booking sync, it temporarily stops Yanolja packages selling, to prevent overbooking.
    > - While paused, selling on Yanolja is halted for the length of the VCMS sync window. Once the Yanolja system error clears, the paused packages go back on sale by themselves.
    > - If the same class of system error keeps recurring, the package is stopped automatically to prevent overbooking.
</Warning>

<Check>
  When a channel integration problem occurs, we notify the contact your property registered, by SMS or KakaoTalk.

  - With no notification contact registered, enter a phone number under VCMS Settings \> Accommodation \> Notification contacts.

### How do I tell whether the booking list itself is failing?

<Check>
  Change the query on the Yanolja booking list, by switching to day view or changing the date range in detailed search, and the system error screen appears.

  ![Yanolja booking list error](/images/image-10.png)

  ![Yanolja booking list error detail](/images/image-9.png)
</Check>

### When will booking sync recover?

<Check>
  The system error comes from outside, so there is no exact recovery time. What we have observed so far:

  - Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, 09:00 to 22:00
  - Unstable sync lasting up to 6 to 8 hours
</Check>

### Yanolja recovered and then broke again.

<Check>
  If the delay returns, you may get another booking sync delay notification.

  When you do, check whether the Yanolja Partner Center booking list is failing again.
</Check>

### Reporting the Yanolja system error

<Check>
  Report intermittent Yanolja Partner Center errors to Yanolja customer support.
</Check>

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Contact Yanolja customer support (1644-1346)">
    - Report the error you see when opening the Yanolja Partner Center booking screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. What to have ready">
    - Business registration number
    - Accommodation name
    - A screenshot of the PC screen showing the error, with the whole screen visible
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. Recovery">
    - Once the booking sync delay clears, the Yanolja Partner Center booking search opens again and VCMS booking sync recovers with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
