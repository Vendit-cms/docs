## What to check when the connection drops

### Check you are signed in to the Chrome extension

<Accordion title="Check the sign-in state on the screen where the Chrome extension is installed." defaultOpen>
  - Look at the Chrome window with the pinned Airbnb or Expedia tab on the left.
    <Columns cols={2}>
      <Column>
        <Frame>
          ![Airbnb sign-in](/images/airbnb-sign-in-2.png "Airbnb sign-in")
        </Frame>
      </Column>
      <Column>
        <Frame>
          ![Expedia Partner Central](/images/expedia-partner-center-1.png "Expedia Partner Central")
        </Frame>
      </Column>
    </Columns>
  - Signed out? Sign back in.
  - After signing in, leave the tab open. Minimizing the window is fine.
    <Info>
      The tab has to stay open for sync with Vendit to hold.
    </Info>
    <Warning>
      The pinned tab on the left exists for the integration. Do not work in it. To check something on the channel, open a new tab.
    </Warning>
</Accordion>

### Check the Vendit extension is switched on

<Accordion title="Is the Vendit extension enabled?" defaultOpen>
  Check the extensions installed in Chrome.

  <Frame>
    ![Chrome extension enabled](/images/chrome-extension-1.png "Chrome extension enabled")
  </Frame>
</Accordion>

### Check the Windows power options

<Accordion title="Is sleep or turn-off-screen set in the Windows power options?" defaultOpen>
  <Info>
    Set the power options for the Windows version you run.
  </Info>

  <Accordion title="Windows 10 power settings" icon="computer" iconType="regular">

      <Step title='Windows icon → Settings → "System".'>
        <Frame>

      </Step>
      <Step title="Check the additional power settings.">
        <Frame>
          ![Windows 10 additional power settings](/images/image-37.png)

      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Windows 11 power settings" icon="computer" iconType="regular">

        <Frame>

### Check the network at your property

<Accordion title="Is the property network working?" defaultOpen>
  If everything above checks out and it still will not connect, look at the network at your property.
</Accordion>
