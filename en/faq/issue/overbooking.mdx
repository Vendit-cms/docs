title: "I have an overbooking."
description: "Check the following first."
---

<Check>
  For a stable integration, VCMS syncs booking data with the channels every minute, and inventory and rates every 5 minutes.

  When an overbooking happens, send the details below to the **Vendit customer experience team (1644-5442)** so it can be traced.

  We come back to you within 2 business days.

  - Booking source
  - Guest name
  - Booking number
  - Check-in and check-out dates and times
  - Sales channel package name
</Check>

<Info>
  Where the cause turns out to be simultaneous bookings inside the 5-minute window, or a booking problem from misconfigured inventory or rates, **compensation is not available.**
</Info>
