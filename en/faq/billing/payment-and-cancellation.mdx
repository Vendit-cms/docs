## Can I pay by bank transfer or cash?

> You can pay for VCMS by card only. You can register both personal and corporate cards.

<Info>
  Your VCMS subscription is paid automatically with the registered card on each recurring payment date. Payments that come up during a subscription, such as Add room, are also processed with the same card.
</Info>

1. On the Subscription screen, click the `Add payment card` button.
2. Choose a personal card or a corporate card and enter the details. For a personal card, enter the card number and expiration date, then complete identity verification. For a corporate card, you only enter the card number, expiration date and business registration number. There is no separate verification.

<Note>
  You can register one default payment method and up to 4 backup cards. Automatic payments use the default payment method only, so set a card with a sufficient limit as your default payment method.
</Note>

## Can I use Booking sync only?

> Yes. Booking sync is free, so you can use only Booking sync without subscribing to Inventory and Rate.

<Check>
  Booking sync is the basic service. If you do not subscribe to Inventory and Rate, you use Booking sync only. You can subscribe to Inventory and Rate and add them whenever you need them.
</Check>

<Info>
  **To leave out only some room types**

  In Settings > Room types, change that room type's Management type to `Managed in channel` and save.

  - `Managed in CMS`: Bookings, inventory and rates for mapped channel packages sync with VCMS. Rates do not sync for rental-type channels such as Liveanywhere, Banlife Stay and Camperest, or for properties on the new Onda Pension Plus rate plan.
  - `Managed in channel`: Only bookings sync, and the accommodation manages inventory and rates directly.
</Info>

<Warning>
  If you use Booking sync only, VCMS does not adjust channel inventory. When a booking comes in, set Stop sell yourself on your other channels. Otherwise, overbooking can occur.
</Warning>

<Note>
  If you subscribe to Inventory later, check that you changed the room type's Management type to `Managed in CMS`. If you leave it as `Managed in channel`, that room type stays on Booking sync only.
</Note>

## Does sync stop if a payment fails?

> Yes. If a payment is missed, channel sync stops. When you pay again, the subscription reactivates right away.

<Warning>
  While sync is stopped, the inventory, rates and Sell status you change in VCMS do not reach your channels, so overbooking can occur.
</Warning>

1. On the Subscription screen, click the `Add payment card` button and add the card you want to pay with. If the card is over its limit or expired, register a different card.
2. Click the new card's `Edit` button and change it to your default payment method. The repeat payment uses the default payment method.
3. Click the button in the notice at the top of the screen to make the payment. If the payment does not go through, check the card limit and balance, then try again.
4. In Subscription > Payment history, check that the payment you just made succeeded.

<Info>
  If you pay again, you pay for the period from the moment you pay through to the next recurring payment date. From then on, payment is made on the original recurring payment date.
</Info>

<Note>
  When sync restarts, the values in VCMS Distribution are applied to your channels. Check the bookings that came in while sync was stopped, and adjust Availability in Distribution to match the actual numbers.
</Note>

If this does not solve the problem, click the Support button and report it.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

> You can cancel it yourself on the Subscription screen. After you cancel, you can keep using Booking sync for free.

<Check>
  When you cancel, the paid features Inventory and Rate stop, and channel bookings keep coming into VCMS. After you cancel, manage inventory and rates directly in each channel.
</Check>

1. On the Subscription screen, check your Subscribed services.
2. Choose the service to cancel and how to cancel it. If you use both Inventory and Rate, you can also end Rate only and switch to using Inventory only.

- `Use until next payment date, then cancel the subscription`: You use the remaining period, and the subscription is canceled on the next payment date.
- `Cancel subscription immediately`: The subscription is canceled as soon as you request it, and the amount for the remaining period is refunded to your existing payment method. If the refund amount is 0 won, the status changes to scheduled for cancellation and you can use the service until the next payment date.

<Warning>
  **Check before you cancel immediately.**

  - If you immediately cancel Inventory, channel sync stops right away and overbooking can occur.
  - If you immediately cancel Rate, rate changes you make in VCMS are not applied to your channels.
  - If you immediately cancel Additional notification contact, notifications no longer go to additional contacts, other than the default contact.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Refund policy**

  - Monthly subscription: If you cancel within 7 days of the payment date, the amount minus the period used is refunded. After 7 days, there is no refund and you can use the service until the next payment date.
  - Annual subscription: If you cancel within 7 days, the amount minus the period used is refunded, based on the discounted price. After 7 days, the remaining period is refunded, based on the amount without the Annual subscription discount.
  - A canceled subscription cannot be restored. To use it again, start a new subscription.
</Note>
