---
title: "After a missed payment, how is the Subscription billed when I pay again?"
description: "If you pay again after a missed payment, you are billed the Subscription for the period from the moment you pay through to the next recurring payment date."
---

<Info>
  Once the payment goes through, the subscription suspended for non-payment reactivates, and from then on payment is made on the original recurring payment date.
</Info>
