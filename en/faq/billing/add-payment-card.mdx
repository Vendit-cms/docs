---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Subscription screen.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the register payment card button.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose personal or corporate when registering.">
    <Check>
      Both are supported.

      1. Personal card: enter the card number and expiry, then complete identity verification.
      2. Corporate card: enter the card number, expiry, and business registration number. No separate verification.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>
