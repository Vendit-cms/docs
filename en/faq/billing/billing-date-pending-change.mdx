---
title: "Can I change the payment date while some services are scheduled for cancellation?"
description: "No. While a change or cancellation is scheduled for the next payment date, the payment date is locked until that change takes effect."
---

<Warning>
  You keep the current subscription until the next payment date, and the change applies from the following subscription period. Until then the subscription items have not changed yet and sit in a 'change pending' state, so you cannot change the payment date until the change takes effect.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Cancel some services immediately and you can change the payment date. You are refunded the amount for the unused period, and the subscription info updates on the spot. Because the subscription info updates right away, you can change the payment date with it.
</Info>
