## What happens to my monthly subscription when the number of rooms changes?

> The monthly subscription is calculated from the number of rooms. If you add rooms while subscribed, they apply after you pay for the added rooms.

<Check>
  - On a paid subscription: When you add rooms, you pay an additional amount for the period left until the next payment date, and the rooms apply after that. This payment appears separately in Payment history as an `Add room` item.
  - On a free trial: Rooms are added right away with no payment, and the first payment after the trial reflects the increased number of rooms.
</Check>

1. In Settings > Room types, select the room type you want to add rooms to.
2. Click the `+ Add` button to add rooms.
3. When the additional payment notice appears, check the amount and pay.

<Info>
  To take a room out of use for a while for construction or maintenance, don't delete the room. Change its room status to Inactive. Inactive rooms are removed from sellable inventory, and they go back on sale when you change the status back.
</Info>

<Note>
  To find out when your subscription amount changes after you reduce rooms, ask using the Support button.
</Note>

## What is the rate after the free trial ends?

> When the trial ends, payment is based on the services you applied for and the number of rooms. If you subscribe during the trial, your service continues without interruption.

<Check>
  **Rates after the trial (VAT excluded)**

  - Inventory only: KRW 5,000 per room per month, base rate of KRW 50,000 per month up to 10 rooms
  - Inventory and Rate together: KRW 10,000 per room per month, base rate of KRW 100,000 per month up to 10 rooms
  - Booking sync and one default Notification contact are free.
</Check>

1. Click the `Free trial period` button at the top of the screen.
2. Add payment card on the Subscription screen.
3. Choose the services to subscribe to and the payment option (`Annual payment` or `Monthly payment`), then apply. Even if you apply during the trial, payment is made when the trial ends.

<Warning>
  Adding a payment card and applying for a subscription are separate steps. Adding a card alone does not start a subscription.
</Warning>

<Note>
  A KakaoTalk or SMS notification goes to the contact registered for your accommodation at these points: one day before payment, Free trial ended and Completed. Annual payment gets a 10% discount, and if you use other VENDIT services, you can get a bundle discount.
</Note>

## How much is the Annual payment discount?

> With Annual payment, you pay for one year at once and get a 10% discount. With Monthly payment, payment is made on the set payment date each month.

<Check>
  You can choose the payment option separately for each service, such as Inventory and Rate. If you choose Annual payment, a 10% discount applies to the payment amount.
</Check>

<Info>
  Even with a discount, the payment amount does not go below the base rate. So for properties with 10 rooms or fewer, the amount for one year equals 12 months of the base rate, even with Annual payment.
</Info>

1. Start your subscription application on the Subscription screen. If you are on a free trial, you can start from the `Free trial period` button at the top of the screen.
2. Choose `Annual payment` or `Monthly payment` for each service.
3. Check the payment amount and discount amount, then apply.

<Warning>
  - While subscribed, you can't switch directly from Monthly payment to Annual payment. Cancel your current subscription, then subscribe again. If you choose Cancel subscription immediately for Inventory, channel sync stops right away.
  - If you cancel an Annual payment subscription within 7 days of the payment date, you get a refund of the discounted amount minus the period you used. After 7 days, the remaining period is refunded at the amount minus the annual discount.
</Warning>

## How much is the monthly subscription per room?

> With Inventory only, it is KRW 5,000 per room per month. With Inventory and Rate together, it is KRW 10,000 per room per month (VAT excluded).

<Check>
  - Booking sync: Free
  - Inventory only: KRW 5,000 per room per month (base rate of KRW 50,000 per month up to 10 rooms)
  - Inventory and Rate together: KRW 10,000 per room per month (base rate of KRW 100,000 per month up to 10 rooms)
  - Additional notification contact: KRW 5,000 each (one default contact is free)
</Check>

<Info>
  All amounts are VAT excluded, and the actual payment amount includes 10% VAT. KRW 5,000 per room plus VAT is KRW 5,500.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Examples (VAT excluded)**

  - 8 rooms, Inventory only: base rate of KRW 50,000 per month
  - 20 rooms, Inventory only: KRW 100,000 per month
  - 30 rooms, Inventory and Rate together: KRW 300,000 per month (KRW 330,000 VAT included)
</Note>

If you choose Annual payment, a 10% discount applies, and if you use other VENDIT services, you can get a bundle discount. No discount takes the amount below the base rate.

## What is the monthly rate with Inventory only?

> If you subscribe to Inventory only, it is KRW 5,000 per room per month, with a base rate of KRW 50,000 per month up to 10 rooms (VAT excluded).

- 8 rooms: base rate of KRW 50,000 per month
- 20 rooms: KRW 100,000 per month
- 40 rooms: KRW 200,000 per month

<Info>
  With Inventory only, rate changes you make in VCMS are not sent to channels. Manage rates directly in each channel. VCMS also does not automatically sell a channel's unconfigured packages, so set the sales start and rate for new packages in the channel.
</Info>

1. On the Subscription screen, change Subscribed services to use Inventory only. If you are subscribing for the first time, select only Inventory in the subscription application window.
2. Choose how to end Rate (`Cancel after using until the next payment date` or `Cancel subscription immediately`).

<Warning>
  If you choose Cancel subscription immediately for Rate, channel rate sync stops right away. If you have Naver connected, stopping Rate can affect your Naver rate settings, so check the Naver article first.
</Warning>

## Do I pay extra if I connect more channels?

> The VCMS subscription is calculated from the number of rooms and the services you subscribe to. It stays the same even if the number of connected channels grows.

<Check>
  The VCMS subscription is calculated from the number of rooms, and Booking sync is a basic service, so it is free. There is also no separate cost for the first connection.
</Check>

<Info>
  The sales commission a channel takes on each booking depends on the contract between the property and that channel, and is paid separately from the VCMS subscription. To raise the sell rate only on channels with high commissions, adjust it with the Final channel rate.
</Info>

## Is there a discount if I use a kiosk or cloud PMS?

> Yes. If you also use other VENDIT services, you can get a bundle discount on your VCMS subscription.

<Check>
  The Discount rate depends on the services you use and your contract terms. Ask using the Support button.
</Check>

<Info>
  Even with a discount, the payment amount does not go below the base rate (Inventory only KRW 50,000 per month, Inventory and Rate together KRW 100,000 per month, VAT excluded). If you have few rooms, the actual reduction can be small.
</Info>

1. Open Payment history on the Subscription screen.
2. Click a payment and check the discount amount in Payment details.

## How are discounts applied?

> There is an Annual payment discount and a bundle discount. No discount takes the amount below the base rate.

<Check>
  - Annual payment: If you pay for one year at once, a 10% discount applies to the payment amount.
  - Bundle discount: You can get this if you use other VENDIT services. Ask about the Discount rate using the Support button.
</Check>

<Info>
  Even with a discount, the payment amount does not go below the base rate that applies up to 10 rooms (Inventory only KRW 50,000 per month, Inventory and Rate together KRW 100,000 per month, VAT excluded). So for properties with few rooms, the discount amount can show as KRW 0.
</Info>

In Subscription > Payment history, click a payment to check the payment amount and discount amount in Payment details.

## Do I pay only the monthly subscription, with no sales commission?

> Yes. VCMS does not take a commission on each booking. You pay only the monthly subscription based on the number of rooms.

<Check>
  **VCMS costs (VAT excluded)**

  - Booking sync: Free
  - Inventory only subscription: KRW 5,000 per room per month (KRW 50,000 per month up to 10 rooms)
  - Inventory and Rate together: KRW 10,000 per room per month (KRW 100,000 per month up to 10 rooms)
  - Additional notification contact: KRW 5,000 each (one default contact is free)
</Check>

<Info>
  However many bookings you have, the subscription stays the same if the number of rooms and subscribed services are the same. There is also no separate cost for the first connection. Sales commissions for channels such as Yanolja and Yeogiottae depend on your contract with each channel and are paid separately from the VCMS subscription.
</Info>
