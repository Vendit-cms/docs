## What happens to the monthly subscription when the number of rooms changes?

> The monthly subscription is calculated from the number of rooms. If you add rooms during a subscription, they apply after you pay for the added rooms.

<Check>
  - On a paid subscription: When you add rooms, they apply after you pay an additional amount for the period left until the next payment date. This payment appears separately in Payment history as an `Add room` item.
  - During the free trial: Rooms are added right away with no payment. The first payment after the trial ends reflects the increased number of rooms.
</Check>

1. In Settings > Room types, select the room type you want to add rooms to.
2. Click the `+ Add` button to add rooms.
3. When the additional payment notice appears, check the amount and pay.

<Info>
  To take a room out of service for a short time for construction or maintenance, do not delete the room. Change its status to Inactive. Inactive rooms are removed from the inventory on sale, and they go back on sale when you change the status back.
</Info>

<Note>
  To find out when the subscription changes after you reduce the number of rooms, contact us with the Support button.
</Note>

## What is the rate after the free trial ends?

> When the trial ends, payment is based on the services you applied for and the number of rooms. If you apply for a subscription during the trial, it continues without interruption.

<Check>
  **Rates after the trial (VAT excluded)**

  - Inventory only: KRW 5,000 per room per month, with a base rate of KRW 50,000 per month for up to 10 rooms
  - Inventory and Rate together: KRW 10,000 per room per month, with a base rate of KRW 100,000 per month for up to 10 rooms
  - Booking sync and one default notification contact are free.
</Check>

1. Click the `Free trial period` button at the top of the screen.
2. On the Subscription screen, add a payment card.
3. Choose the services to subscribe to and the payment option (`Annual payment` or `Monthly payment`), then apply. Even if you apply during the trial, payment is made when the trial ends.

<Warning>
  Adding a payment card and applying for a subscription are separate steps. Adding only a card does not start a subscription.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The contact registered for your accommodation gets a KakaoTalk or SMS notification for each of these: one day before payment, free trial ended, and payment completed. Annual payment gets a 10% discount, and if you use other VENDIT solutions, you can get a bundle discount.
</Note>

## How much is the discount for annual payment?

> With Annual payment, you pay for one year at once and get a 10% discount. With Monthly payment, payment is made on the set payment date each month.

<Check>
  You can choose the payment option separately for each service, such as Inventory and Rate. If you choose Annual payment, a 10% discount applies to the payment amount.
</Check>

<Info>
  Even with a discount, the payment amount does not go below the base rate. So for a property with 10 rooms or fewer, the one-year amount with Annual payment is the same as 12 months of the base rate.
</Info>

1. On the Subscription screen, start a subscription application. During the free trial, you can start from the `Free trial period` button at the top of the screen.
2. For each service, choose `Annual payment` or `Monthly payment`.
3. Check the payment amount and the discount amount, then apply.

<Warning>
  - During a subscription, you cannot switch directly from Monthly payment to Annual payment. Cancel the current subscription, then subscribe again. If you cancel Inventory immediately, channel sync stops right away.
  - If you cancel Annual payment within 7 days of the payment date, you get a refund based on the discounted amount, minus the period you used. After 7 days, the remaining period is refunded at an amount that excludes the annual discount.
</Warning>

## How much is the monthly subscription per room?

> With Inventory only, it is KRW 5,000 per room per month. With Inventory and Rate together, it is KRW 10,000 per room per month (VAT excluded).

<Check>
  - Booking sync: Free
  - Inventory only: KRW 5,000 per room per month (base rate of KRW 50,000 per month for up to 10 rooms)
  - Inventory and Rate together: KRW 10,000 per room per month (base rate of KRW 100,000 per month for up to 10 rooms)
  - Additional notification contact: KRW 5,000 each (one default contact is free)
</Check>

<Info>
  All amounts are VAT excluded, and the actual payment amount includes 10% VAT. KRW 5,000 per room plus VAT is KRW 5,500.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Examples (VAT excluded)**

  - 8 rooms, Inventory only: base rate of KRW 50,000 per month
  - 20 rooms, Inventory only: KRW 100,000 per month
  - 30 rooms, Inventory and Rate together: KRW 300,000 per month (KRW 330,000 VAT included)
</Note>

If you choose Annual payment, a 10% discount applies, and if you use other VENDIT solutions, you can get a bundle discount. No discount goes below the base rate.

## What is the monthly rate with Inventory only?

> With an Inventory-only subscription, it is KRW 5,000 per room per month, with a base rate of KRW 50,000 per month for up to 10 rooms (VAT excluded).

- 8 rooms: base rate of KRW 50,000 per month
- 20 rooms: KRW 100,000 per month
- 40 rooms: KRW 200,000 per month

<Info>
  With Inventory only, changing rates in VCMS does not update them in channels. Manage rates directly in each channel. VCMS also does not automatically sell channel packages that are not set up, so set the sales start and rate for new packages in the channel.
</Info>

1. On the Subscription screen, change Subscribed services to use Inventory only. If you are subscribing for the first time, select only Inventory in the subscription application window.
2. Choose how to end Rate (`Use until next payment date, then cancel the subscription` or `Cancel subscription immediately`).

<Warning>
  If you cancel Rate immediately, channel rate sync stops right away. If you are connected to Naver, stopping Rate can affect your Naver rate settings, so check the Naver document first.
</Warning>

## Do I pay more if I connect more channels?

> The VCMS subscription is calculated from the number of rooms and the services you subscribe to. If you connect more channels, the VCMS subscription stays the same.

<Check>
  The VCMS subscription is calculated from the number of rooms, and Booking sync is a basic service, so it is free. There is also no separate cost when you first connect.
</Check>

<Info>
  The sales commission a channel takes on each booking depends on the contract between the property and each channel, and it is paid separately from the VCMS subscription. To raise the Sell rate only on channels with high commissions, you can adjust it with Final channel rate.
</Info>

## Do I get a discount if I use a kiosk or cloud PMS?

> Yes. If you also use other VENDIT solutions, you can get a bundle discount on your VCMS subscription.

<Check>
  The Discount rate depends on the solutions you use and your contract terms. Contact us with the Support button.
</Check>

<Info>
  Even with a discount, the payment amount does not go below the base rate (KRW 50,000 per month for Inventory only, KRW 100,000 per month for Inventory and Rate together, VAT excluded). With few rooms, the actual reduction may be small.
</Info>

1. On the Subscription screen, open Payment history.
2. Click a payment and check the discount amount in Payment details.

## How are discounts applied?

> There are Annual payment discounts and bundle discounts. No discount goes below the base rate.

<Check>
  - Annual payment: If you pay for one year at once, a 10% discount applies to the payment amount.
  - Bundle discount: Available if you use other VENDIT solutions. To find out the Discount rate, contact us with the Support button.
</Check>

<Info>
  Even with a discount, the payment amount does not go below the base rate that applies for up to 10 rooms (KRW 50,000 per month for Inventory only, KRW 100,000 per month for Inventory and Rate together, VAT excluded). So for a property with few rooms, the discount amount may show as KRW 0.
</Info>

In Subscription > Payment history, click a payment to see the payment amount and discount amount in Payment details.

## Do I pay only the monthly subscription, with no sales commission?

> Yes. VCMS does not take a commission on each booking. You pay only the monthly subscription based on the number of rooms.

<Check>
  **What you pay VCMS (VAT excluded)**

  - Booking sync: Free
  - Inventory only subscription: KRW 5,000 per room per month (KRW 50,000 per month for up to 10 rooms)
  - Inventory and Rate together subscription: KRW 10,000 per room per month (KRW 100,000 per month for up to 10 rooms)
  - Additional notification contact: KRW 5,000 each (one default contact is free)
</Check>

<Info>
  However many bookings you have, the subscription stays the same as long as the number of rooms and the subscribed services are the same. There is also no separate cost when you first connect. Sales commissions from channels such as Yanolja and Yeogiottae depend on your contract with each channel and are paid separately from the VCMS subscription.
</Info>
