## How do I invite staff or family members to my account?

> In Settings > Users, you can invite people by phone number or email and set their role and permissions.

1. In Settings > Users, click `Invite users`.
2. Set the invitation method (Phone number or Email), the contact, and the role, then add the person to the list of people to invite.
   The role can be Admin, Manager, or Staff. You can invite up to 10 people at a time.
3. If needed, set the permissions and language.
   If you invite someone as View only, they can see the screens but cannot change anything. If you change the language, the invitation SMS or email goes out in that language.
4. The process is complete when the invited person opens the link in the SMS or email and accepts the invitation.
   Once they accept, the accommodation is added to their My accommodations list.

<Info>
  **Managing users after you invite them**

  - In the Users list, click the option button on the right and use Edit info to change a user's role and detailed permissions (View, Write, Edit, Delete).
  - For staff who have left, use the same option button to Remove them. You can only remove users whose role is the same as or lower than yours.
</Info>

<Note>
  Inviting someone as a user does not send them disconnection notifications. To have them receive notifications too, add their number to Notification contacts in Settings > Accommodation. Payment is required for any contact beyond the 1 included by default.
</Note>

## I received an invitation. How do I sign up?

> Open the invitation link, sign up with Email, Phone number, or Google, and then accept the invitation.

1. Click the link in the invitation email or SMS.
   On a PC, we recommend opening it in Chrome.
2. Choose a sign-in method.
   With Email, you get a verification email. With Phone number, you get a verification code by KakaoTalk or SMS. With Google, click `Continue with Google` and choose your account.
3. If you are signing up for the first time, complete verification and then set a password.
   Enter a password that meets the requirements shown on the screen.
4. Finish signing up and accept the invitation.
   Once you accept, the accommodation is added to your My accommodations list.

<Info>
  This password is only for VCMS. It does not need to match your channel or email password. If you already have a VCMS account, you do not need to sign up again. Sign in with that account and accept the invitation.
</Info>

<Note>
  If you sign up with a different email or phone number from the one that was invited, you will not see the invitation. Sign up with the contact that was invited, or ask to be invited again using the contact you signed up with.
</Note>

## The invitation email or SMS did not arrive.

> Check the contact for typos and check your spam folder. If it is still not there, the Admin should invite you again using the same contact.

1. Check where the invitation would arrive.
   For email, check your inbox, spam folder, and promotions folder. It can arrive a few minutes late. For a phone number, check your SMS messages, your SMS spam folder, and your blocked-numbers list.
2. Ask the Admin who invited you to check in Settings > Users that the contact they entered is correct.
3. If the contact is correct, the Admin should invite you again using the same contact.
   Inviting again sends the invitation SMS or email one more time.
4. If the contact is wrong, the Admin should send a new invitation to the correct contact.

<Info>
  **You were invited but cannot see the accommodation**

  - Check that you signed in with an account that uses the same email or phone number the invitation was sent to.
  - The accommodation is added to your My accommodations list only after you accept the invitation from the link in the SMS or email.
</Info>

If this does not solve the problem, click the Support button and report it.
