## I cannot sign in to VCMS.

> Check your sign-in method, whether the verification code arrived, and whether you accepted the invitation, in that order.

1. Check that you are signing in with the method you used to sign up.
   You sign in to VCMS with an email, a phone number, or a Google account. If you signed up with Google, click `Continue with Google` and choose the same Google account.
2. If you do not remember your password, sign in with a verification code.
3. If the verification code or verification email does not arrive, check where it was sent.
   For email, check the address and your spam folder. For phone, check the number, KakaoTalk, your SMS spam folder, and your blocked-numbers list.
4. If you see 'No connected accommodations' after you sign in, check that you accepted the invitation.
   Accept it from the link in the invitation SMS or email. You must sign in with the account that matches the contact you were invited with.

<Note>
  You cannot sign in if you enter a channel password, such as your Yanolja or Yeogiottae password, in the VCMS Password field. The two passwords belong to different accounts.
</Note>

If this does not solve the problem, click the Support button to report it.

## Can I use VCMS on mobile?

> Yes, you can use VCMS on mobile. However, there is no dedicated mobile app, and you can use it mainly for inventory and rate adjustment in Distribution.

<Check>
  Go to https://vcms.io in a mobile browser to adjust inventory and rates. Other settings have limited functionality, so use them on a PC. If you sign in with the same account on another PC, you see the same content.
</Check>

**Features available on PC and mobile**

- **Distribution**: Sell status, Availability, and Rate are available on both PC and mobile. Check-in and check-out times, Bulk update, View settings, and filters are available on PC only.
- **Bookings**: Card view is available on both PC and mobile. List view and View settings are available on PC only, and only some filters are available on mobile.
- **Settings**: Channels are available on both PC and mobile. Room types, Packages, Rates, Inclusions, Periods, Timetables, Accommodation, Users, and Integrations are available on PC only.

<Warning>
  You cannot fix an Airbnb or Expedia connection from a phone or another PC. Sign in again in the pinned Chrome tab on the PC at your business where the extension is installed.
</Warning>

## I forgot my ID or password.

> Your VCMS ID is the email or phone number you used to sign up. You can sign in with a verification code even if you do not know your password.

1. On the sign-in screen, enter the phone number you used to sign up.
   Enter the verification code you receive by KakaoTalk or SMS to sign in.
2. If you cannot verify with your phone number, enter the email you used to sign up.
   Check the verification email sent to you. If you do not see it, check your spam folder.

<Info>
  **If you do not know the email or phone number you used to sign up**

  - Ask an Admin of the same accommodation to check your phone number and email in Settings > Users.
  - If you cannot use either one, ask the Admin to invite you again with the contact you use now.
</Info>

<Note>
  Your VCMS password is separate from channel passwords, such as your Yanolja or Yeogiottae password. If you change a channel password to get into VCMS, that channel is disconnected.
</Note>

If this does not solve the problem, click the Support button to report it.

## Can I manage multiple accommodations with one account?

> Yes, you can connect multiple accommodations to one account and switch between them in 'My accommodations'.

1. In My accommodations, choose the accommodation you want to manage.
   When you choose one, you go straight to that accommodation. If a name is cut off, hover over it to see the full name.
2. To create a new accommodation, use the button at the top right of the list screen.
3. If someone else created the accommodation, ask its Admin to invite you, then accept the invitation.
   Once you accept, it is added to My accommodations.

<Info>
  Channel connections, inventory and rates, users, and notification contacts are set separately for each accommodation. You can have a different role (Admin, Manager, or Staff) at each accommodation.
</Info>

<Note>
  When you sign in to a channel, always check that the property name shown on the screen is correct. If you sign in from another accommodation's screen, the wrong property gets connected.
</Note>
