## Do bookings from my own website (booking engine) sync with VCMS?

> Bookings from your property's own website don't sync with VCMS. Only Yeogiottae Partner Booking Hub bookings come in, through Booking sync.

<Info>
  A website your property runs itself or a booking engine from a separate vendor isn't one of the channels VCMS integrates with. Website bookings don't come into VCMS, and VCMS inventory doesn't go down.
</Info>

1. When a website booking comes in, go to `Distribution`, reduce the availability for that date or set it to Stop sell, and click `Save` at the top.
2. If rooms sell on other channels and none are left, close sales on your website yourself.

<Note>
  **Yeogiottae Booking Hub bookings**

  Yeogiottae Partner Booking Hub bookings come in under a package with [HOMEPAGE] in front of the channel package name. When one first comes in, go to Settings > Channel > Connected channels > Channel packages and map it to a VCMS package. You can't adjust rates or inventory in VCMS. Only bookings sync.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Bookings from sales channels not connected to VCMS can lead to overbooking if you don't enter them yourself. Enter each booking as soon as it comes in.
</Warning>

## Can I manage day use inventory and rates?

> VCMS currently manages inventory and rates for overnight packages only.

<Check>
  Day use supports Booking sync only. Day use inventory and rate features are planned for later. We'll let you know separately when they're added.
</Check>

- Change Day use sales hours, rates, and closing directly on each channel's admin screen. Setting an overnight package to Stop sell in `Distribution` doesn't set day use to Stop sell.
- Day use bookings come into Bookings. To see only day use bookings, choose Day use under Stay type in the filter.

<Info>
  If you use the VENDIT VCLOUD service, day use channel packages that aren't mapped to a VCMS day use package are created under an unassigned room type. Map day use channel packages to VCMS day use packages too.
</Info>

## Can I sync inventory only, without rates?

> Yes. If you subscribe to Inventory only, or turn off rate sync for individual channel packages, only inventory and bookings sync. You manage rates on each channel.

<Info>
  Booking sync is free. For a paid subscription, you can choose Inventory only, or Inventory and Rate together. You can't use Rate on its own.
</Info>

1. To sync only inventory for the whole property, select Inventory only in Subscription.
2. To manage rates on the channel for specific channel packages only, go to Settings > Channel > Connected channels > Channel packages, open that package's `Sync settings`, turn off `Rate`, and save.

<Warning>
  If you use Inventory only, VCMS doesn't automatically open unconfigured packages on the channel. Unconfigured packages are packages you haven't listed for sale on the channel yet. First set the rates and when sales open on the channel.
</Warning>

## Do Expedia, Bookingdotcom, and Airbnb sync too?

> Airbnb and Expedia sync bookings, inventory, and rates. Bookingdotcom syncs bookings only.

<Info>
  You connect Airbnb and Expedia by installing the VCMS extension in Chrome on your property's PC. Bookings, inventory, and rates sync every 5 minutes. To keep syncing, Chrome with the extension installed, the channel tab you're signed in to, and your property's PC must all stay on.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Bookingdotcom**

  Bookingdotcom syncs bookings only. Inventory and rate changes you make in VCMS don't reach Bookingdotcom, so manage them directly in the extranet. When a room sells on another channel, set Bookingdotcom to Stop sell yourself.
</Note>

## What does each channel sync, including inventory and rates?

> Most channels sync bookings, inventory, and rates. Only some channels and special channel packages differ.

- **Bookings, inventory, and rates all sync**: Connectable channels not listed separately below. This includes Airbnb and Expedia.
- **Rate adjustment not available (bookings and inventory sync only)**: Liveanywhere, Banlife Stay, Camperest, and properties using a new rate plan on Onda Pension Plus
- **Channel packages that sync bookings only**: Yeogiottae Partner Booking Hub packages ([HOMEPAGE]), manually created Yanolja bookings ([MANUAL]), Yanolja hard block packages, and day use packages
- On Airbnb, Ddnayodotcom, and Yanolja pension, the sales quantity of one channel package is limited to 1 at most.

<Warning>
  Yanolja hard block packages are packages the sales channel buys rooms for in advance and resells, so you can't adjust their inventory or rates. Don't map hard block channel packages to a VCMS package. Leave them unmapped.
</Warning>

1. Go to Settings > Channel > Connected channels > Channel packages and check the inventory and rate sync badges for each package.
2. To change what syncs for a channel package, click it and turn `Inventory` and `Rate` on or off.
3. To manage a whole room type yourself, go to Settings > Room types and change Management type to `Managed in channel`.
   When you change to Managed in channel, the channel packages connected to that room type sync bookings only.

## What syncs with Bookingdotcom?

> Bookingdotcom syncs only bookings to VCMS. Manage inventory and rates directly in the Bookingdotcom extranet.

<Info>
  Bookingdotcom bookings come into VCMS Bookings, but inventory and rate changes you make in VCMS don't reach Bookingdotcom. Even when a booking from another channel reduces VCMS inventory, Bookingdotcom isn't set to Stop sell automatically.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Bookingdotcom isn't connected to VCMS inventory, so its overbooking risk is higher than on other channels. On dates with few rooms left, reduce the sales quantity in the extranet in advance.
</Warning>

<Check>
  **Bookings with 2 or more rooms**

  Bookings that need a check of Booking details require extra verification under Bookingdotcom's security policy, and may not come in automatically. Be sure to check these bookings in the extranet.
</Check>
