## Yeogiottae got disconnected after I changed the password

> When you change your password on Yeogiottae, it no longer matches the password saved in VCMS, and the connection drops. Enter the new password in VCMS to reconnect.

<Info>
  Yeogiottae asks you to change your password periodically. Even with the correct password, sign-in is blocked while Yeogiottae requires a change.
</Info>

1. Sign in directly to the Yeogiottae app or the Yeogiottae Partner site with your VCMS integration account.
   If a password change notice appears, follow it to change your password.
2. For Yeogiottae Partner accounts, check that two-factor authentication is off.
   Yeogiottae Partner accounts with two-factor authentication on get disconnected from VCMS.
3. In VCMS, go to Settings > Channel, click Yeogiottae, check the account details, and reconnect with the new password.
   Make sure the connected ID is the integration account for this property.

<Warning>
  If you keep trying to connect with the old password, Yeogiottae's sign-in policy blocks the account from connecting for 5 minutes. First check that the new password signs you in on the Yeogiottae site, then enter it.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **If that doesn't fix it, click the Support button and report the following.**

  - The property name and channel type (Yeogiottae app or Yeogiottae Partner)
  - The Yeogiottae ID connected to VCMS
  - The date and time of the disconnection, and whether you recently changed the password
  - A screenshot of the error message you get when you reconnect
</Note>

## How do I get an additional Yeogiottae Partner account (multiple sign-in)?

> You can request an additional account on the Yeogiottae Partner website, under Partner support > 1:1 customer inquiry.

<Info>
  A Yeogiottae Partner account can't be signed in from two places at the same time. If you sign in to Yeogiottae Partner directly with the account used for the VCMS integration, you may be signed out repeatedly. Keep separate accounts: one for the VCMS integration and one for your own use.
</Info>

1. On the Yeogiottae Partner website, go to Partner support > 1:1 customer inquiry and request an additional account.
   Follow Yeogiottae's guidance on the issuing criteria and process.
2. Choose one of the two accounts for the VCMS integration only. Then, in VCMS, go to Settings > Channel and check that the account connected to Yeogiottae is that account.
3. You and your staff should sign in to Yeogiottae Partner with the other account, not the integration-only account.

<Warning>
  Yeogiottae Partner accounts with two-factor authentication on get disconnected from VCMS. Two-factor authentication may be on by default for a new account, so turn it off.
</Warning>
