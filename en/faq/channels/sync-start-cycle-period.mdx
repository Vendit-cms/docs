## Does turning on sync reopen rooms I blocked on the channel?

> Yes, it can. When you turn on sync, channel values change to match the values in VCMS Distribution.

<Info>
  While syncing, VCMS `Distribution` is the reference. VCMS can't see dates you set to Stop sell directly in the channel's partner center, so if a date is on sale in VCMS, the channel sells it again. If you change a value directly on the channel while syncing, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes.
</Info>

1. Check which dates you set to Stop sell directly on the channel and why (phone bookings, group bookings, construction, and so on).
2. For phone or group bookings, reduce the Availability for those dates in `Distribution`.
3. For dates you won't sell, set Stop sell in `Distribution` and click `Save` at the top.
4. Check Availability, rates, and Sell status again, then start sync.

<Note>
  For properties that use Inventory without Rate, VCMS doesn't automatically open packages that aren't set up on the channel. On Naver, syncing inventory and rates together deletes Today's Deal and Holidays schedules.
</Note>

## How many days can I sync with a channel?

> You can set the channel Sync period to up to 180 days. You can choose 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, or 180 days. Inventory and rates sync to your sales channels for the period you set.

1. Go to Settings > Accommodation > Sync period settings.
2. Select the period you want.
   The Sync period moves forward one day every day. If you set it to 30 days, 30 days starting today are open, and one more day opens the next day.

<Note>
  If a sales channel limits its sales period, the channel's policy takes priority.
</Note>

<Info>
  **What happens if I shorten the Sync period?**

  Booking, inventory, and rate sync stops for dates beyond the shortened period. Dates already open on the channel don't change to Stop sell automatically. Before you shorten the period, set Stop sell in `Distribution` for dates you won't sell.
</Info>

<Info>
  **What happens if I extend the Sync period?**

  On some sales channels, packages with no rate or inventory set yet may show as available for sale. Before you extend the period, check the rates and inventory for the dates that will open in `Distribution`.
</Info>

<Warning>
  If you keep a channel's sales period open longer than the Sync period, VCMS doesn't send rates for dates outside the Sync period, so those dates may sell at old rates. For those dates, set Stop sell directly on the channel or check the rates.
</Warning>

## How often do channels sync?

> VCMS syncs booking info with channels every minute, and inventory and rates every 5 minutes.

<Info>
  Changes saved in `Distribution` and inventory reduced by new bookings go to channels right away, without waiting for the interval. VCMS also rechecks all dates every 5 minutes. If a channel value is different, VCMS changes it to match the VCMS value.
</Info>

- Airbnb, Expedia: Bookings, inventory, and rates all sync every 5 minutes.
- Yanolja: Only bookings within 4 days, including today, come in every minute. Bookings for later dates can come in up to about 10 minutes late.

<Check>
  **If your changes don't reach the channel, check the following**

  - Whether you saved your changes with `Save` at the top
  - Whether `Distribution` is in Sync stopped status. While sync is stopped, it's Booking sync only, and inventory and rates don't go to channels.
</Check>

<Note>
  If you change a value directly on the channel while syncing, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes. To exclude only specific channel packages, go to Settings > Channel > Connected channels > Channel packages and turn off `Inventory` and `Rate`. To exclude a whole room type, go to Settings > Room types and change Management type to `Managed in channel`.
</Note>
