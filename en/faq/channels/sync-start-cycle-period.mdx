## Does turning on sync reopen rooms I closed on the channel?

> Yes, it can. When you turn on sync, channel values are matched to the VCMS Distribution values.

<Info>
  While syncing, VCMS `Distribution` is the reference. VCMS can't see dates you set to Stop sell directly in the channel's partner center, so if a date is on sale in VCMS, it goes back on sale on the channel too. If you change a value directly on the channel while syncing, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes.
</Info>

1. Check which dates you set to Stop sell directly on the channel, and why (phone bookings, group bookings, construction, and so on).
2. For phone or group bookings, lower Availability for those dates in `Distribution`.
3. For dates you won't sell, set Stop sell in `Distribution` and click `Save` at the top.
4. Check Availability, rates, and Sell status again, then start sync.

<Note>
  For properties that use Inventory without Rate, VCMS doesn't automatically open packages that aren't set up on the channel. On Naver, syncing inventory and rates together deletes Today's Deal and Holidays schedules.
</Note>

## How far ahead can a channel sync?

> You can set the channel sync period to up to 180 days. Pick 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, or 180 days. Inventory and rates sync to the sales channel for the period you set.

1. Go to Settings > Accommodation > Sync period.
2. Select the period you want.
   The sync period moves forward one day every day. If you set it to 30 days, 30 days from today are open, and the next day one more day opens.

<Note>
  Where a sales channel limits its own selling window, the channel's policy wins.
</Note>

<Info>
  **What happens if I shorten the sync period?**

  Bookings, inventory, and rates stop syncing for dates beyond the shortened period. Dates already open on the channel don't change to Stop sell automatically. Before you shorten the period, set Stop sell in `Distribution` for dates you won't sell.
</Info>

<Info>
  **What happens if I extend the sync period?**

  On some sales channels, packages that have no rate or inventory set yet can appear as available to book. Before you extend the period, check the rates and inventory for the newly opened dates in `Distribution`.
</Info>

<Warning>
  If you keep a channel's selling window open longer than the sync period, VCMS doesn't send rates for dates outside the sync period, so those dates can sell at old rates. For those dates, set Stop sell directly on the channel or check the rates.
</Warning>

## How often do channels sync?

> VCMS syncs Booking info with channels every minute, and inventory and rates every 5 minutes.

<Info>
  Changes saved in `Distribution` and inventory reduced by new bookings go to the channel right away, without waiting for the cycle. VCMS also rechecks all dates every 5 minutes. If a channel value differs, VCMS changes it to match the VCMS value.
</Info>

- Airbnb, Expedia: Bookings, inventory, and rates all sync every 5 minutes.
- Yanolja: Only bookings within 4 days, including today, come in every minute. Bookings for later dates can arrive up to about 10 minutes late.

<Check>
  **If changes don't appear on the channel, check the following**

  - Whether you saved the change with `Save` at the top
  - Whether `Distribution` is set to Sync stopped. While sync is stopped, only bookings sync. Inventory and rates don't go to the channel.
</Check>

<Note>
  If you change a value directly on the channel while syncing, it returns to the VCMS value within 5 minutes. To exclude only certain channel packages, turn off `Inventory` and `Rate` under Settings > Channels > Connected channels > Channel packages. To exclude a whole room type, change Management type to `Managed in channel` under Settings > Room types.
</Note>
