title: "Agoda rates aren't being applied"
description: "What to check when Agoda rates aren't applied: occupancy settings, and the difference between booking amounts and displayed prices."
---

## Rates set in VCMS aren't applied on Agoda

> First check that the VCMS package mapped to Agoda has Occupancy-based pricing, and that its base occupancy and max occupancy match YCS.

<Info>
  Agoda takes rates as Occupancy-based pricing. If the mapped VCMS package has no Occupancy-based pricing, or its occupancy settings differ from the YCS room, rates and Stop sell aren't applied. Without a rate, the YCS calendar shows only the number of rooms.
</Info>

1. Under Settings > Packages, open the package mapped to Agoda and check that Occupancy-based pricing is set.
   If you don't add an extra rate, you can set the extra rate per guest to 0 KRW.
2. Check that the package's base occupancy and max occupancy match the occupancy under Property > Room settings in YCS.
3. Under Settings > Channels > Connected channels > Channel packages, check that the Agoda package is mapped to the right VCMS package.
   Also check that `Rate` isn't turned off on the channel package, and that Sync at the top of `Distribution` is on.
4. In YCS, check the rate plan link and the Sell status.
   Check the rate plan link under Rates & bookings settings > Rate plan settings in YCS, and the Sell status under Property > Room settings. You can't sell while the status is Pending sale.

<Note>
  Derived packages with a link icon on the YCS calendar can't have their rates changed in YCS either. VENDIT can't edit information that you can't edit on the channel yourself.
</Note>

If this doesn't solve the problem, press the Support button to report it.

## Agoda base occupancy and max occupancy differ from VCMS

> Make the occupancy in YCS room settings and the base occupancy and max occupancy of the VCMS package the same. If they differ, rates may not be sent.

<Info>
  Agoda supports Occupancy-based pricing, so when guests exceed the base occupancy, an extra rate per guest is added to the Room rate. If the Occupancy-based pricing VCMS sends doesn't match the YCS room occupancy, rates aren't applied.
</Info>

1. Under Property > Room settings in YCS, check the occupancy of each room.
2. Under Settings > Packages in VCMS, open the package mapped to Agoda, enter the same base occupancy and max occupancy as YCS in Occupancy-based pricing, and press `Save`.
   If you don't add an extra rate, set the extra rate per guest to 0 KRW.

<Warning>
  If you change a room type's max occupancy in VCMS, the base occupancy and max occupancy of its connected packages adjust automatically. After you edit a room type, check again that the package occupancy matches YCS.
</Warning>

<Note>
  - VCMS supports Occupancy-based pricing for adults only. Set child rates directly in YCS.
  - If you map the same package to a channel that doesn't support Occupancy-based pricing (Yanolja, Yeogiottae, Coolstay, Naver, Ddnayodotcom, Onda, Tripbtoz), the rate for max occupancy applies. If you don't want this, split it into separate packages.
</Note>

## Agoda booking amount differs from the guest's Payment amount

> Agoda bookings come into VCMS at the Net rate. This can differ from the amount the guest paid.

<Info>
  Agoda is an official integration, so VCMS can only receive the information Agoda sends. The booking amount Agoda sends is the Net rate. So the VCMS booking amount is closer to what the property will receive than to what the guest paid.
</Info>

1. Check the booking amount in VCMS Bookings and compare it with the amount of the same booking in YCS.
2. If you want to manage by Sell rate, set `Commission` for the Agoda property under Settings > Channels > Property info.
   For when it applies, pick either `Time of setting` or `Today's check-in`.

<Note>
  With Agoda, promotions and contract terms mean amounts don't come out exactly by the commission. Treat amounts calculated with Commission as a reference only. Commission is used only on incoming booking amounts, so the rates sent to Agoda don't change.
</Note>

## Agoda shows a lower price than the rate you set

> A YCS promotion may be applied. Check your promotions and, if needed, mark up the Agoda rate with Final channel rate.

<Info>
  VCMS sends Distribution rates to Agoda, but Agoda decides the final price guests see. If a promotion is running in YCS, the discount is applied to the rate VCMS sent. YCS promotions are managed in YCS, separately from VCMS.
</Info>

1. Check whether any promotions are running in YCS.
2. To keep the promotion and still match the price, add a markup weight to the Agoda `Final channel rate` under Settings > Channels > Property info.
3. To stop Agoda sales on specific dates only, change the Agoda channel package for those dates to Stop sell in `Distribution` and press `Save`.
   If Stop sell isn't applied on Agoda, check that the mapped VCMS package has Occupancy-based pricing, and that its base occupancy and max occupancy match YCS room settings.

<Warning>
  If you use Stop sell at the room type or package level, sales on other channels stop too. To stop Agoda only, select the Agoda channel package and change that.
</Warning>

If this doesn't solve the problem, press the Support button to report it.
