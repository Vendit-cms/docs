---
title: "How far ahead can a channel sync?"
description: "Up to 180 days. Pick it in 30-day steps (30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180) and inventory and rates sync to the sales channel for that far out."
---

<Info>
  The sync window is changed under \[Settings\] - \[Accommodation\] - \[Sync window\].
</Info>

<Note>
  Where a sales channel limits its own selling window, the channel's policy wins.
</Note>

<Info>
  **What happens if I shorten the sync window?**

  Bookings, inventory, and rates stop syncing for dates beyond the shortened window. Manage those dates on the channel directly from then on.
</Info>

<Info>
  **What happens if I extend it?**

  On some sales channels, packages that have no rate or inventory set yet can appear as available to book. Get those settings in place first, in line with how you operate.
</Info>
