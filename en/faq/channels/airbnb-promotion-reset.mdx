<Warning>
  Connecting Airbnb and VCMS resets the promotions already set on Airbnb.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<Accordion title="Are rule combinations kept?">
  Yes. These survive the connection.

  | Rule combination | What it does |
  | --- | --- |
  | Last-minute discount | Lowers the nightly rate as the check-in date approaches |
  | Early bird discount | Gives guests booking well ahead an early booking discount |

<Accordion title="What if I want to keep using promotions?">
  Turn off rate sync in VCMS and manage rates on Airbnb yourself.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="VCMS → Settings → Channels → Airbnb → channel packages.">
    </Step>
    <Step title="Turn off rate sync on every Airbnb package.">
    </Step>
    <Step title="Keep inventory management in VCMS, and manage rates on Airbnb.">
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>
