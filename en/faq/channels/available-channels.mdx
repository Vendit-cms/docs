## FAQ

<Accordion title="Do both YCS and Agoda Homes connect?">
  Agoda runs two partner sites, YCS and Agoda Homes. Only YCS is officially supported, so an Agoda Homes account cannot connect to VCMS. Create a YCS account with Agoda and use that.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between the Yeogi Eottae owner app and the Partner Center?">
  The owner app is the sales admin site for motels. The Partner Center manages hotels, pensions, and the like. One exception: the Partner Center's `Home & Villa` category does not connect to VCMS.
</Accordion>
