## Booking status

Bookings are grouped by where they are in the process.

<Frame>
  ![Booking Default](/images/en-bookings-default.png)
</Frame>

- **All**: every booking, whatever its status.
- **Completed**: the booking has synced.
- **Cancelled**: the booking was cancelled.
- **Assigned**: the booking was assigned to a room in the connected integration (VCLOUD).
- **Pending**: the booking has not synced yet and is waiting.

## View settings

Choose which columns appear and reorder them however you like.

### Shown by default

- **Booking check**: whether the booking details have been reviewed
- **Stay type**: overnight or day use
- **Booking status**: completed, cancelled, assigned, or pending
- **Check-in**: scheduled check-in date and time
- **Check-out**: scheduled check-out date and time
- **Nights**: length of stay
- **Total guests**: number of guests on the booking
  <Warning>
    Yanolja, Yeogiottae, Naver, and Coolstay do not send guest counts, so their bookings show 1 guest.
  </Warning>
- **Source**: where the booking came from
- **Integration**: the connected integration
- **Guest**: name of the lead guest
- **Room type**: room type on the synced booking
- **Booking number**: the channel's booking number
- **Quantity**: number of rooms booked
- **Phone number**: guest phone number
- **Sales amount**: the sell rate on the connected channel
- **Memo**: a note written by your property, editable from the booking detail
- **Synced at**: when the booking synced

### Hidden by default

- **Paid at**: when the booking was paid
- **Request**: what the guest wrote when booking
- **Package**: the VCMS package linked to the channel package
- **Channel package**: the channel package on the booking
- **Net amount**: the channel's net rate
- **Outstanding amount**: what the channel still owes
- **Transport**: how the guest is arriving
- **Cancelled at**: when the booking was cancelled
- **Adults, children, infants**: guest counts by age group

## Filters

Filters narrow the list to the bookings you want.

- **Date**: pick the dates to look at.
- **Date basis**: stay period, check-in, check-out, or synced at.
- **Stay type**: overnight or day use.
- **Channel**: only connected channels appear.
- **Room type**: filter by room type.
- **Package**: filter by package.

## Booking detail

Click a booking and the detail panel opens, split into **Booking info** and **Change history**.

### Booking info

The booking's details, grouped by topic. For example:

- **Guest info**: stay type, booking status, guest, phone number
- **Sync info**: source, booking number, synced at
- **Stay period**: check-in, check-out, nights
- **Booking info**: room type, channel package, package, quantity, total guests, transport
- **Request and memo**
- **Payment info**: sales amount, net amount, amount due, paid at
- **Booking check**

### Change history

Who changed what, and when. Each entry shows the changed field as "before → after" alongside the timestamp.

## CSV download

Bookings download as a CSV. Press **CSV download** above the list and every booking matching the filters you have applied (stay date range and the rest) is saved to the file.

<Frame>
  ![Booking CSV Download](/images/en-booking-csv-download.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  The CSV holds only the bookings currently loaded on screen. Load 30 of 50 and you download 30. To get them all, scroll to the end of the list so every booking loads, then download again.
</Info>
