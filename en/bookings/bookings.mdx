## Booking status

Bookings are grouped by where they are in the process.

<Frame>
  ![Booking Default](/images/en-bookings-default.png)
</Frame>

- **All**: every booking in any status, including booked, canceled, assigned, and pending.
- **Booked**: the booking has synced.
- **Canceled**: the booking was canceled.
- **Assigned**: the booking was assigned to a room in the integration (VCLOUD).
- **Pending**: the booking has not synced yet and is waiting.

## View settings

Choose which columns appear and reorder them however you like.

### Shown by default

- **Confirmed**: whether the booking details have been reviewed
- **Stay type**: the booking's stay type (for example, overnight or day use)
- **Status**: the booking's status (booked, canceled, assigned, or pending)
- **Check-in**: scheduled check-in date and time
- **Check-out**: scheduled check-out date and time
- **Nights**: length of stay
- **Guests**: number of guests on the booking
  <Warning>
    Yanolja, Yeogiottae, Naver, and Coolstay do not send guest counts, so their bookings show 1 guest.
  </Warning>
- **Source**: where the synced booking came from
- **Integration**: the connected integration
- **Guest name**: name of the lead guest
- **Room type**: room type on the synced booking
- **Booking ID**: the channel's booking number for the synced booking
- **Quantity**: quantity on the synced booking
- **Phone number**: guest phone number
- **Sales amount**: the sell rate on the connected channel
- **Memo**: a memo recorded by the accommodation, editable in Booking details
- **Synced at**: when the booking synced

### Hidden by default

- **Paid at**: when the booking was paid
- **Request**: what the guest wrote when booking
- **Package**: the VCMS package mapped to the channel package
- **Channel package**: the channel package on the synced booking
- **Net amount**: the net rate on the connected channel
- **Amount due**: the amount still outstanding from the connected channel
- **Transport type**: how the guest on the synced booking is arriving
- **Canceled at**: when the synced booking was canceled
- **Adults, children, infants**: guest counts by age group

## Filters

Filters narrow the list to the bookings you want.

- **Date**: pick the dates to look at.
- **Date type**: choose stay period, check-in, check-out, or synced at.
- **Stay type**: choose overnight or day use.
- **Channel**: only connected channels appear.
- **Room type**: filter by room type.
- **Package**: filter by package.

## Booking details

Click a booking in the list and the Booking details panel opens. It has two tabs: **Booking info** and **Change history**.

### Booking info

The booking's details, grouped by topic. For example:

- **Guest information**: stay type, status, guest name, phone number
- **Integration information**: source, Booking ID, synced at
- **Stay period**: check-in, check-out, nights
- **Booking info**: room type, channel package, package, quantity, guests, transport type
- **Request and memo: Request, Memo**
- **Payment info: Sales amount, Net amount, Amount due, Paid at**
- **Confirmed**

### Change history

Who changed what, and when. Each entry shows the changed field as "before → after" alongside the timestamp.

## Download CSV

Bookings download as a CSV. Press **Download CSV** at the top right of the list and every booking matching the filters you have applied (stay date range and the rest) is saved to the file.

<Frame>
  ![Booking CSV Download](/images/en-booking-csv-download-2.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  The CSV holds only the bookings currently loaded on screen. Load 30 of 50 and you download 30. To get them all, scroll to the end of the list so every booking loads, then download again.
</Info>
