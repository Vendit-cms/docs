## FAQ

### Why do I keep getting signed out of Yeogiottae Partner?

<Info>
  **One Yeogiottae Partner account cannot be signed in from two places at once**

  If you sign in to Yeogiottae Partner yourself with the account VCMS uses for the integration, the same account is in use in two places, and you can be signed out repeatedly.

  For stable use, keep **the account VCMS connects with separate from the account you sign in with**.

  You can request an extra account through **the Yeogiottae Partner site \> Partner support \> 1:1 inquiry**. For the detailed policy, follow Yeogiottae's guidance.
</Info>
