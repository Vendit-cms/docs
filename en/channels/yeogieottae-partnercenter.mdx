## FAQ

### Why do I keep getting signed out of the Yeogiottae Partner Center?

<Info>
  **One Yeogiottae Partner Center account cannot be signed in from two places at once**

  - Sign in to the Partner Center yourself with the account VCMS uses for the integration and the same account is in use in two places, which drops the session repeatedly.
  - Keep **the account VCMS connects with separate from the account you sign in with**.
  - Request an extra account through **the Yeogiottae Partner Center site \> Partner support \> 1:1 inquiry**. Their own policy applies.
</Info>
