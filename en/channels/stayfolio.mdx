## Integration notes

### Only Overnight packages are synced

Stayfolio sells Overnight packages only. Day use packages are not synced. Settings you make on the Day use tab in VCMS Distribution do not carry over to Stayfolio.

---

### Set your rates in Stayfolio

Stayfolio does not support rate adjustment by date. If you adjust rates in VCMS, the change does not carry over to Stayfolio.

<Info>
  Set the rates you sell on Stayfolio directly in Stayfolio. In VCMS, you can manage inventory and bookings.
</Info>

---

### Blocking and opening rooms is handled as an '예약불가' booking

When you block a room in VCMS, a booking named '예약불가' is created in Stayfolio. When you open the room again, that booking is canceled.

<Info>
  An '예약불가' booking in your Stayfolio booking list is not a real guest booking. It means the inventory is blocked.
</Info>

---

### Inventory is not adjusted for rooms with bookings

Rooms booked by real guests are excluded from inventory control. Even if you increase inventory manually in VCMS, bookings that already came in are protected from cancellation.

---

### Some Booking info is not provided

The Booking info sent from Stayfolio does not include the items below.

- **Amount**: Only the Sell rate is provided.
- **Arrival method**: There is no information, so it shows as 'On foot'.
- **Check-in and check-out time**: There is no information, so the booking is created with the times set in your VCMS Timetable.
