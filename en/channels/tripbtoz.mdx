## Things to watch

### Rates cannot go below the Tripbtoz minimum

Tripbtoz sets a minimum selling price per room. However you adjust the rate in VCMS, it cannot land below the minimum set on Tripbtoz.

<Info>
  The minimum is set and checked in **Tripbtoz Partner Center \> Rooms and rate plans**. To sell lower, lower the sell rate on Tripbtoz first.
</Info>
