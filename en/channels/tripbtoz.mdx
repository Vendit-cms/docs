## Frequently asked questions

### Do you keep getting signed out of Tripbtoz?

<Info>
  **Tripbtoz does not let one account be signed in from two places at once**

  If you sign in to the Tripbtoz partner page directly with the account VCMS uses for its connection, the same account is in use in two places. You may be signed out again and again.

  For stable use, keep **the account for the VCMS connection and the account you use yourself** separate.
</Info>

---

## Tripbtoz connection notes

### Rates cannot go below the Tripbtoz minimum amount

Tripbtoz sets a minimum sales amount per room. However you adjust the rate in VCMS, it cannot land below the minimum amount set on Tripbtoz.

<Info>
  The minimum amount is set and checked in **Tripbtoz Partner Center \> Rooms and rate plans**. To sell at a lower rate, adjust the 'Sell rate' on Tripbtoz first.
</Info>
