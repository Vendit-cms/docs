## Things to watch

### **Social login accounts cannot connect**

A Liveanywhere account signed up through social login cannot connect to VCMS. Use a phone number or email account.

---

### Set rates on Liveanywhere directly

VCMS cannot adjust Liveanywhere rates. Use it for inventory and booking sync, and set and manage rates on the channel itself.

<Warning>
  Change the rate of a Liveanywhere package in VCMS and nothing reaches the channel.\
  When a rate has to change, change it on Liveanywhere.
</Warning>

---

### Liveanywhere runs on rental contracts

Unlike ordinary accommodation bookings, Liveanywhere works in **6-night or 30-night rental contracts**. What syncs is the contract, not an individual guest's booking.

<Info>
  Booking information appears per rental contract rather than per guest.
</Info>

---

### Closing single dates can trigger a minimum-nights shortfall

<Frame>
  ![Liveanywhere minimum nights shortfall](/images/리브애니웨어-최소박수미달-1.png)
</Frame>

Close individual dates and the dates between them can show as unbookable for **falling short of the minimum nights**. Close the 13th and the 15th, for instance, and the 14th in between is blocked.
