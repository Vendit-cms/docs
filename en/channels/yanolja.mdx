## Connect Yanolja

In Settings > Channel > Yanolja, enter your Yanolja partner center ID and password. Agree to 'Collection and use of personal (credit) information', then click **Sign in** to connect.

  ![Yanolja Connect](/images/yanolja-2fa-1-connect.png "Yanolja Connect")

## Yanolja two-factor authentication

<Warning>
  Starting in November 2026, Yanolja requires two-factor authentication. To connect or reconnect Yanolja, complete two-factor authentication by following the steps below. Your Yanolja integration stays paused until you finish verification.
</Warning>

### Step 1. Reconnect and check the notification

If Yanolja is disconnected, click **Reconnect** in Settings > Channel > Yanolja. Then click the 'Channel account two-factor authentication is required' message in the notifications at the top right of the screen or in KakaoTalk.

![Yanolja notification](/images/notification.png)

### Step 2. Verify with your phone number

Click **Get verification code** to send a code to the mobile phone number or email address registered with Yanolja. Enter the code you receive by text or email, then click **Verify**.

### Step 3. Verification complete

You are done when the screen shows 'Verification complete'. Your Yanolja integration restarts soon, and you can close this window.
