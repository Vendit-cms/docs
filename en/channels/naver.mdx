## Connecting Naver

To connect Naver to VCMS, **turn off Naver's two-step verification** first, then connect with your existing Naver ID and password.

<Warning>
  If the Naver connection has dropped, reconnect in the order below.
</Warning>

### Step 1. Turn off Naver two-step verification

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Naver app, menu (≡) at the top left \> your name \> **Two-step verification** \> re-enter your password \> **Turn off** at the bottom of the page

    <Columns cols={3}>
      <Column>
        <Frame>
          ![Naver 2FA Deactivate 1](/images/naver-2FA-deactivate-1.png "Naver 2FA Deactivate 1")
        </Frame>
      </Column>
      <Column>
        <Frame>
          ![Naver 2FA Deactivate 2](/images/naver-2FA-deactivate-3.png "Naver 2FA Deactivate 2")
        </Frame>
      </Column>
      <Column>
        <Frame>
          ![Naver 2FA Deactivate 3](/images/naver-2FA-deactivate-2-1.png "Naver 2FA Deactivate 3")
        </Frame>
      </Column>
    </Columns>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PC">
    Naver ID \> Security settings \> Basic security \> **Two-step verification** \> re-enter your password \> cancel two-step verification \> **\[Turn off two-step verification completely and sign in with ID and password only\]** \> **Turn off**

    <Frame>
      ![Naver 2FA Deactivate Web](/images/naver-2FA-deactivate-web.png "Naver 2FA Deactivate Web")
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  For more on turning it off 👉 see [Naver's help page on two-step verification](https://help.naver.com/service/5640/contents/1853?lang=ko&osType=COMMONOS).
</Info>

### Step 2. Turn off Naver's login blocking

Under Naver **Security settings \> Login block settings**, "Block logins from other regions" stops VCMS from connecting. Turn that off too.

<Frame>
  ![Naver Connect](/images/naver-connect.png "Naver Connect")
</Frame>

### Step 3. Reconnect Naver in VCMS

VCMS \> Settings \> Channels \> Naver \> **Reconnect** \> enter ID and password

<Info>
  **Worth knowing**

  - Turning off two-step verification deletes every application password you created.
  - Turn two-step verification back on after connecting and the connection drops.
</Info>

## Things to watch

<Info>
  Take care if your Naver room list has Today's Deal (a set discount applied if the room has not sold by 00:00) or holiday schedules registered.

  - Syncing inventory and rates deletes Today's Deal and the holiday schedules, replacing them with the inventory and rates from the VCMS Distribution screen.
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-5.png"
        alt="Naver room list"
        style={{ width:"77%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
</Info>

<Warning>
  Syncing inventory only leaves the rate information deleted, so packages can go unsold on the holiday dates you set, or sell at some older rate you registered earlier.
</Warning>
