### Set rates on Camperest directly

Camperest rates cannot be adjusted from VCMS. Use it for inventory and booking sync, and set and manage rates on the Camperest channel itself.

<Warning>
  Change the rate of a Camperest package in VCMS and nothing reaches the channel.\
  When a rate has to change, change it on Camperest.
</Warning>
