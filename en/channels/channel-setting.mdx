## Connected channel details

Click a connected channel to see its account details and the last sync time.

<Frame>
  ![Channel property details](/images/채널_숙소정보.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  **Property connection state**

  - Sets whether the channel property is connected.
  - While it is not connected, bookings, inventory, and rates stop syncing for that property.
  - Property ID: the property's unique number, used internally by Vendit.
</Info>

## Channel commission

For sales channels that carry no sell rate and net rate of their own, set a commission and VCMS works out the booking's sell rate and net rate.

<Frame>
  ![Channel commission](/images/채널_예약수수료율.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  Commission applies either from the moment you set it, or against today's check-in date. You choose.
</Info>

## Final channel rate

Adjusts the rate that leaves the Distribution screen for each channel.

<Frame>
  ![Final channel rate](/images/채널_채널전송가.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  **When is the final channel rate useful?**

  When you want the offer to look equally attractive across channels. Say Agoda lets you run promotions freely while Yanolja and Yeogiottae make the promotion you want hard to set up. The final channel rate is how you land the same price on every sales channel.
</Info>

## Managing channel packages

Link a channel's packages to VCMS, or unlink them.

<Frame>
  ![Channel packages](/images/채널_채널상품.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  **Channel package settings**

  - **Max inventory**: for a channel package with a fixed quantity, cap it individually.
  - **Final channel rate**: adjusts the rate for one channel package only, on top of what Distribution applied.
  - **Sync settings**: turn inventory and rate sync on or off for a single channel package.
  - **Inventory management**: whether the channel package syncs inventory.
  - **Rate management**: whether the channel package syncs rates.
  - **Unmap the channel package**: its inventory and rates stop being adjusted, and it becomes a package that is not connected to VCMS.
</Info>

<Frame>
  ![Editing a channel package](/images/채널_채널상품_정보수정.png)
</Frame>
