## Things to watch

### Set rates on Banlife Stay directly

Banlife Stay rates cannot be adjusted from VCMS. Use it for inventory and booking sync, and set and manage rates on the channel itself.

<Warning>
  Change the rate of a Banlife Stay package in VCMS and nothing reaches the channel.\
  When a rate has to change, always change it on Banlife Stay directly.
</Warning>
