## Things to watch

### Set rates on Banryeo Life Stay directly

Banryeo Life Stay rates cannot be adjusted from VCMS. Use it for inventory and booking sync, and set and manage rates on the channel itself.

<Warning>
  Change the rate of a Banryeo Life Stay package in VCMS and nothing reaches the channel.\
  When a rate has to change, change it on Banryeo Life Stay.
</Warning>
