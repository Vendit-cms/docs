## Things to watch

### Up to 50 rates per room type

<Frame>
  ![Camfit rate limit](/images/camfit-1-1.png)
</Frame>

Channel policy caps a room type at 50 registered rates. So however you adjust rates in VCMS, at most 50 per room type make it across.

<Warning>
  Go past 50 and only the first 50, counting from the nearest date, are applied. Rates for the dates after that are not set.
</Warning>

---

### VCMS single-night rates apply to multi-night stays too

<Frame>
  ![Camfit multi-night rates](/images/camfit-2.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  VCMS has no separate multi-night rate. Even with multi-night rates entered on Camfit, the VCMS single-night rate is what applies.
</Info>
