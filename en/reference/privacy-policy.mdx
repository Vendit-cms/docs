Note that because Customers are corporations or sole proprietors, the Personal Information Protection Act generally does not apply to them. Where it does apply, the Company applies the Personal Information Protection Act and this Privacy Policy.

1. Purpose of processing personal information and items processed

   Purpose of processing: Membership registration and management, and providing, maintaining, repairing, and giving guidance on the Solution.

   Personal information processed: User ID, password, business registration number, business name, address, business type, business category, contact person’s name, contact person’s email, contact person’s phone number, digital certificate ID, digital certificate password, and OTA registration details.
1. Provision of personal information to third parties

   The Company uses Customers’ personal information only within the scope stated above. Without Customers’ prior consent, the Company does not use it beyond that scope and, in principle, does not disclose Customers’ personal information externally.

   Note that, under Article 15(3) and Article 17(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act and Article 14-2 of its Enforcement Decree, the Company may use or provide personal information without consent after considering whether it is related to the original purpose of collection, whether additional use or provision is foreseeable given the circumstances of collection or processing practices, whether it unfairly infringes the interests of the data subject, and whether necessary safety measures such as pseudonymization or encryption have been taken.

1. Retention and usage period of personal information

   Until five years have passed from the date of membership termination. However, where related laws require retention, the Company keeps member information for the period those laws set. In this case, the Company uses the retained information only for the purpose of retention, and the retention periods are as follows.
   - Website visit records (login records): 3 months; other communication confirmation data: 12 months
   - Reason for retention: Article 15-2(2) of the Protection of Communications Secrets Act and Article 41(2) of its Enforcement Decree
2. Destruction procedure and methods

   In principle, Customers’ personal information is destroyed without delay once the retention period expires or the purpose of collection and use is achieved.

   The Company’s destruction procedure and methods are as follows.

   (1) Destruction procedure

   The Company identifies personal information for which a reason for destruction has arisen and destroys it with the approval of the Company’s Data Protection Officer.

   (2) Destruction methods
   - Personal information printed on paper is shredded or incinerated.
   - Personal information stored as electronic files is deleted using technical methods that make the records unrecoverable.
3. Rights of Customers and how to exercise them

   Customers may at any time exercise rights such as requesting access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of their registered personal information, and may terminate their membership or request withdrawal of consent to collection. To access, correct, delete, or suspend processing of personal information, or to withdraw consent to collection, contact the Data Protection Officer in writing, by phone, or by email. The Company will act without delay.
4. Installation, operation, and refusal of automated personal information collection devices

   The Company uses cookies, which store Customers’ information and retrieve it from time to time, to provide personalized and customized services.

   Cookies are very small text files that the server used to run a website sends to the Customer’s browser. They are stored on the hard disk of the Customer’s computer. When the Customer later visits the website, the website server reads the cookies stored on the Customer’s hard disk to keep the Customer’s settings and provide customized services.

   Cookies do not automatically or actively collect information that identifies individuals. Customers can refuse or delete cookies at any time.

   The Company uses cookies to “prevent excessive applications, and provide normal access paths and the job application service.”

   To set whether to allow cookies (in Internet Explorer):

   At the top of the web browser, [Tools] -\> [Internet Options] [Privacy]

   If you refuse cookies, it may be difficult to provide general services.
5. Technical and administrative safeguards for personal information

   In handling Customers’ personal information, the Company takes the following technical and administrative measures to keep personal information from being lost, stolen, leaked, altered, or damaged.

   A. Measures against hacking: The Company does its best to prevent members’ personal information from being leaked or damaged by hacking or computer viruses. The Company backs up data regularly in case personal information is damaged, uses up-to-date antivirus software to keep Customers’ personal information and data from being leaked or damaged, and uses encrypted communication to transmit personal information securely over networks. The Company also controls unauthorized access from outside and works to put in place every possible technical measure to keep the system secure.

   B. Minimizing and training staff who handle personal information: The Company limits access permissions to personal information to Company employees, contractors, and agents who need to know the information to process it on the Company’s behalf, and limits handling staff to the people in charge. They are bound by strict confidentiality obligations under contract, and if they breach these obligations, they may face sanctions or their contracts may be terminated. The Company assigns separate passwords for access to personal information and renews them regularly. The Company always stresses compliance with the Privacy Policy through regular training for the people in charge.
