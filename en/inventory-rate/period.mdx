### Period list

<Check>
  **The periods that drive rate-by-period.**

  - Search by the month, weekday, or day you want.
  - Reorder periods freely.
  - When periods overlap, the one higher in the list (the higher priority) applies first. A package's own period order can be brought in line with this priority through "Sort periods" on [overnight packages](/en/inventory-rate/package-stay).
</Check>

![Period list](/images/기간_1.png)

### Creating a period

<Check>
  **Add the period you want.**

  - **Period name (required)**
  - **End date**
    - Run the period indefinitely, or give it a start and end date to use it temporarily.
  - **Months**
    - Pick any months from January to December.
  - **Weekdays**
    - Pick any days from Sunday to Saturday.
  - **Days**
    - Pick any dates from the 1st to the 31st.
</Check>

![Creating a period](/images/기간_2\(1\).png)

### Period rate adjustment

![Period rate adjustment](/images/기간_3.png)

<Check>
  **Adjust the rate across the whole period, by amount or by percentage.**

  - Turn period rate adjustment on and rates flex by period.
  - Mark up or discount per currency.
  - Adjustment methods
    - By amount
    - By percentage
    - By percentage, then an extra amount on top
</Check>
