---
title: "Periods"
description: "Package rates can differ by month, by weekday, and by day of the month, set through periods."
---

### Period list

<Check>
  **The periods that drive rate-by-period.**

  - Search by the month, weekday, or day you want.
  - Reorder periods freely.
  - When periods overlap, the one higher in the list (the higher priority) applies first. A package's own period order can be brought in line with this priority through 'Align period order' on [Overnight package](/en/inventory-rate/package-stay).
</Check>

![Period list](/images/en-period-list.png)

### Creating a period

<Check>
  **Add the period you want.**

  - **Period name** **(\*required)**
  - **End date**
    - Run the period indefinitely, or give it a start and end date to use it temporarily.
  - **Select months**
    - Pick any months from January to December.
  - **Select days**
    - Pick any days from Sunday to Saturday.
  - **Select days of the month**
    - Pick any dates from the 1st to the 31st.
</Check>

![Creating a period](/images/en-period-create.png)

### Period rate adjustment

![Period rate adjustment](/images/en-period-rate-adjust.png)

<Check>
  **Adjust the rate across the whole period, by amount or by percent.**

  - Turn period rate adjustment on and rates flex by period.
  - Mark up or discount per currency.
  - Rate adjustment methods
    - By amount
    - By percent
    - By percent, then an extra amount on top
</Check>

### Deleting a period

![Deleting a period](/images/en-period-delete-icon.png)

<Check>
  **A period you no longer use can be deleted.**

  - Turn period rate adjustment on and rates flex by period.
  - Mark up or discount per currency.
  - Rate adjustment methods
    - By amount
    - By percent
    - By percent, then an extra amount on top
</Check>

![Period deletion](/images/en-period-delete-confirm.png)
