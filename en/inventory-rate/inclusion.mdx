### Create inclusion

![Creating an inclusion](/images/en-inclusion-create.png)

<Check>
  What goes into an inclusion:

  - **Inclusion name (required)**
  - **Icon**
    - An icon to tell inclusions apart.
  - **Tag**
    - Tags group inclusions together.
  - **Currency**
    - The currency of the inclusion's rate.
  - **Inclusion rate (required)**
    - The amount for a quantity of one.
  - **Fixed rate**
    - For an inclusion bought at a set cost, turn Fixed rate on and it sells at that price.
    - The rate holds, untouched by markups and discounts.
</Check>
