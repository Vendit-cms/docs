### Rate list

![Rate list](/images/요금_5.png)

<Check>
  Manage the rates your packages connect to.

  - See what a rate is made of: the room rate and the inclusions.
  - `Collapse ▲` and `Expand ▼` show or hide a rate's inclusions.
</Check>

![Rate list expanded](/images/요금_4\(1\).png)

### Creating a rate

![Creating a rate](/images/요금_2.png)

<Check>
  Create the rate you price against.

  - **_Rate name (required)_**
  - **Currency**
    - The currency this rate is in.
  - **_Room rate (required)_**
    - The room rate for this rate.
  - **Inclusions on or off**
    - The inclusions that make up the rest of the rate.
    - With inclusions off, the rate is the room rate alone.
  - **Fixed rate**
    - The rate holds, untouched by markups and discounts.
</Check>

### Inclusions

![Rate inclusions](/images/요금_3.png)

<Check>
  Add whatever you include on top of the room price.

  - Set inclusions to `on` and the inclusion picker opens on the right.
  - Create an inclusion, or pick an existing one and set its quantity.
  - Pick an inclusion and its amount is added to the room rate to give the final amount.
</Check>

### Deleting a rate

![Rate connected to a package](/images/요금_7.png)

<Check>
  A rate connected to a package cannot be deleted.
</Check>

![Rate not connected to a package](/images/요금_8.png)

<Check>
  A rate with no package attached can be deleted.
</Check>
