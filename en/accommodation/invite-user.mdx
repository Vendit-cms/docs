## Inviting users

### Adding people to invite

Use \[Invite users\] to bring in an Admin, Manager, or Staff member. Choose the invitation method, enter the person's phone number or email, pick a role, then add them to the pending list.

<Frame>
  ![User 2](/images/사용자_2.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  You can invite up to 10 people at a time.
</Info>

### Permissions and language

You can restrict a user to read-only access, and choose the language they see, at the point of invitation.

### Accepting an invitation

- Invite someone who already has a pending invitation and the SMS or email is simply sent again.
- The person accepts by opening the link in the invitation message or email.
- Once accepted, the accommodation appears in their list of accommodations.
- Cancel the request before it is accepted and that person gets no access.

## Changing a user's role

Select the option button on the right to edit a user's details or remove them.

<Frame>
  ![User 8](/images/사용자_8.png)
</Frame>

### Setting the role

- Edit info lets you change the role: Admin, Manager, or Staff.
- Detailed permissions (Read, Create, Edit, Delete) can be set individually.
- Clear every detailed permission and the user can do nothing but sign in to VCMS.

<Frame>
  ![User 10](/images/사용자_10.png)
</Frame>

---

## Removing a user

### Remove

- You can remove users whose role is the same as yours or lower.
- A removed user has no access to the accommodation until you invite them again.
