## Sign in with email

Enter your email address, then open the verification email we send you.

<Frame>
  ![Sign In Email](/images/Sign-In-Email.png)
</Frame>

<Check>
  Can't find the verification email? Check your spam folder.
</Check>

---

## Sign in with Google

Select \[Continue with Google\]. A popup opens, and you pick the Google account you want to use.

---

## Sign in with a phone number

Enter your phone number, then type in the verification code sent by KakaoTalk or SMS.

<Frame>
  ![Sign In Phone Number](/images/Sign-In-Phone-Number.png)
</Frame>
