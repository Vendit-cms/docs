> **Highlights**<br />"The Support button is easier to spot, and picking dates on mobile is easier."

## Improvements

### The Support button stands out more

The Support button is now more visible, so you can find it right away when you need help. Its size is adjusted to fit web and mobile screens.

<Frame>
  ![Improved support button](/images/260930-support-button-develop-1.png "Improved support button")
</Frame>

---

### See listing info for new partners when you connect the Dida channel

> For properties not on Dida yet

When you connect the Dida channel in Settings > Channels, a banner for new listings appears. <br />Tap the banner to open the onboarding guide page and check the listing steps.

<Frame>
  ![Dida banner](/images/260930-dida-banner-2.png "Dida banner")
</Frame>

---

### Pick dates more easily on mobile

> For properties that check Distribution on mobile

- On mobile, you can pick any date from yesterday to 180 days ahead.
- Tap a date you already picked and it moves to the center of the screen. Scrolling is smoother too.

---

### Spot unread notifications more easily

When you have unread notifications, the dot on the notification icon at the top right shows in a brighter red.

<Frame>
  ![Notification dot](/images/260930-noti-4.png "Notification dot")
</Frame>
