## Improvements

### See rates adjusted by the rate range right away

> For owners who set a minimum and maximum rate range on their packages

On the Distribution rate adjustment screen, you can now see right on the screen when an amount is adjusted because of the rate range. If the adjustment you enter takes a rate outside the package's rate range, the minimum or maximum rate applies instead.

- The notice "Rates outside the rate range are applied at the minimum or maximum rate." appears.
- Minimum and maximum amounts set by the rate range are highlighted, so you can tell at a glance which items were adjusted.

</Frame>

### See the sync status of channel packages that are not connected yet

> For owners who connect and manage channel packages

In Channel \> Channel packages, Inventory and Rate sync badges now appear on packages that are not connected yet. In the package selection menu, a tooltip shows the details.

<Frame>
  ![Sync info on channel packages](/images/260921-channel-added-meta-info.png "Sync info on channel packages")
</Frame>

The category filter in the audit log now includes Tag, Role and Policy. Narrow the log down by the criteria you want.
