## Improved

### See which channel packages cannot take inventory or rates, at link time

<Accordion title="Yanolja promotional packages and manual bookings">
  Yanolja discount packages \[PROMOTION\] and manually created bookings \[MANUAL\] cannot take rate or inventory adjustments. Only bookings sync.

  <Frame>
    ![Yanolja channel package](/images/yanolja-channel-package-1.png)
  </Frame>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Agoda YCS derived packages">
  Agoda derived packages carry a link icon on the YCS calendar, as below. They are tied to another rate plan, and even YCS itself cannot change their rate.

  <Frame>
    <img
      src="/images/agoda-derived-1.png"
      alt="Agoda derived package"
      title="Agoda derived package"
      className="mx-auto"
      style={{ width:"70%" }}
    />
  </Frame>

  <Frame>
    ![Agoda channel package](/images/agoda-channel-package-1.png)
  </Frame>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Yeogi Eottae Partner Center Booking Hub packages">
  Bookings from Booking Hub, the Partner Center's own booking engine, cannot take rate or inventory adjustments. Only bookings sync.

  <Frame>
    ![Yeogi Eottae channel package](/images/yeogi-channel-pacakge-1.png)
  </Frame>
</Accordion>

### Telling whether a channel property is deactivated is easier

  ![No property connected](/images/연결된-숙소-없음-표기.png)
