## New

### Choose how far a stop-sell or resume reaches

> For properties running several packages under one item and changing sell status in bulk

<Frame>
  ![Choosing the scope of a change](/images/하위항목변경.png)
</Frame>

When you stop or resume selling, you now choose how far the change reaches into the items below. It used to cascade automatically. Now there are two options, and you pick the one that fits.

**More detail**

- **Change everything selected**: the items below all move to the same value.
- **Keep manual settings**: items you set by hand keep their settings.

---

### The same choice on mobile

> For properties running Distribution from a phone on site

<Frame>
  ![Scope of change on mobile](/images/하위변경항목.png)
</Frame>

The scope choice available on PC now works the same on mobile. You can pick how far a sell status change reaches while you are away from the desk.

---

### Packages on a fixed rate are protected

> For properties that hold certain packages at a fixed rate

<Frame>
  ![Fixed rate protection](/images/고정요금.png)
</Frame>

Fixed rate has been improved. A package on a fixed rate is protected from stray rate changes on the Distribution screen, and the fixed state is clearer to see.

**More detail**

- A pin (📌) marks fixed-rate packages, so you can tell at a glance which ones they are.
- Changing a daily rate on the Distribution screen leaves the fixed rate alone.
- Fixed rates are excluded from drag-copy and bulk updates.
- Package inclusions cannot be used on a fixed-rate package.

<Frame>
  ![Setting a fixed rate on a package](/images/상품고정요금설정.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Fixed-rate packages cannot use rate periods either. To change the rate, turn fixed rate off first.
</Warning>

<Frame>
  ![Rate period settings](/images/기간별요금설정.png)
</Frame>
