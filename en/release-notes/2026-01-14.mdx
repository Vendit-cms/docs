## New

### Download Bookings search results as CSV

> For properties that analyze booking data elsewhere, or need to keep it

### A clearer sub-item selection screen for Stop sell and Open sell

> For properties managing sell status across several sub-packages at once

When you Stop sell or Open sell an item and the items below it have different sell statuses, the screen that asks how to change them is more direct. You can see at a glance which items are affected, so the change goes through without mistakes.

**More detail**

- Stop sell an item that has packages still selling underneath it and you get a screen asking whether to Stop sell those too.
