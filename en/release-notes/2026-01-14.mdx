## New

### Download booking search results as CSV

> For properties that analyse booking data elsewhere, or need to keep it

<iframe src="https://preview.vendit.co.kr/demo/cmkdypcpl0z0eqm44vxeln9rt?utm_source=link&lang=English" width="100%" height="415" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

Search results on the Bookings screen download as a CSV file. The booking data you need comes straight out, with no manual work, which makes settlement and reporting easier.

### A clearer stop-sell and resume selection screen

> For properties managing sell status across several packages at once

When the items below have different sell statuses, the screen that asks how to change them is more direct. You can see which items are affected, so the change goes through without mistakes.

**More detail**

- Stop selling an item that has packages still selling underneath it and you get a screen asking whether to stop those too.
