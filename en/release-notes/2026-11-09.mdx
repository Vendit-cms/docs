## New features

### Connect the Stayfolio channel

> For properties that sell on Stayfolio

You can now connect the Stayfolio channel in VCMS and manage Inventory and Bookings together.

**Learn more**

- Only Overnight packages are connected. Day use is not supported.
- When you block a room, an 'Unavailable' booking is created. When you open a room, that booking is canceled.
- Rooms that have actual bookings are excluded from Inventory control. Even if you increase Inventory manually in VCMS, bookings that have already come in are not canceled.
- Booking info provides only the Sell rate.
- Bookings have no arrival method information, so they show as On foot.
- Bookings have no check-in or check-out time information, so they are created with the times you set in the VCMS Timetable.

<Warning>
  **Set Rates in Stayfolio**

  Stayfolio does not support daily Rate adjustment. If you adjust Rates in VCMS, the change does not apply to Stayfolio, so set your Rates directly in Stayfolio.
</Warning>
