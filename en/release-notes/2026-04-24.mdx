## **New**

### Airbnb and Expedia sync bookings, inventory, and rates

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Column>
    <Frame>
      ![Airbnb](/images/에어비앤비-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <Frame>
      ![Expedia](/images/익스피디아-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Column>
</Columns>

Booking, inventory, and rate sync has been added for the Airbnb and Expedia channels. The inventory and rates you set in VCMS carry across automatically.

**More detail**

- How to connect: \[Channels\] - \[Available channels\], then click \[Airbnb\] or \[Expedia\]
- Airbnb and Expedia sync bookings, inventory, and rates every 5 minutes.
- The guides have the details ([Airbnb🔗](https://docs.vcms.io/en/channels/airbnb) / [Expedia🔗](https://docs.vcms.io/en/channels/expedia)).

Agoda properties can now be created directly in VCMS.

**More detail**

- How: \[Channels\] - \[Available channels\], then \[Agoda\] - \[banner\]
- The banner does not appear if Agoda is already connected.

<Tip>
  Create the Agoda property through Vendit and the partnership benefit applies automatically. Click the banner to start.
</Tip>

### Import channel packages to pick up channel-side changes

<Frame>
  ![Import channel packages](/images/260423-Import-Channel-Pkg-1.png)

Package information on a connected channel can be pulled in and updated on the spot.

**More detail**

- How: \[Channels\] - \[Connected channels\] - \[the channel\] - \[Property details\] - \[Import channel packages\]

## **Improved**

### The channel page is rebuilt

<Frame>
  ![Rebuilt channel page](/images/260423-Channel-2.png)
</Frame>

The channel list and property detail UI have been reworked, and connecting a channel now happens in a modal.

**More detail**

- Commission and final channel rate are recognised more reliably.
- Channel packages can be mapped while activating the channel property, which makes the flow both easier and steadier.

---

### The subscription page is rebuilt

<Frame>
  ![Rebuilt subscription page](/images/Update-Note-Subscription-1.png)
</Frame>

Subscription features have been reworked from the ground up.

- Subscription information is split by plan, so you see what applies to yours.
- Payment history can be sorted by payment date.

<Info>
  Changing a subscription, changing the payment method, changing the billing date, and adding subscriptions are coming.
</Info>
