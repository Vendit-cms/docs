## New features

### See sold bookings right in Distribution

> For properties that need a quick look at bookings by date

In Distribution, select a Sold count and the bookings for that date open on the right. You can see who is staying and when without going to the Bookings screen.

<Frame>
  ![Distribution sidebar](/images/261016-distribution-sidebar-1.png "Distribution sidebar")
</Frame>

**Learn more**

- See the Guest name, Payment amount, Check-in and Check-out, Nights, Booking ID, and sales channel on one screen.
- Inventory and OCC show for each room type, and the number of bookings shows for each package.
- You can collapse and expand each package. Collapse all and Expand all are also available.
- The most recently synced bookings appear at the top.
- You can also see the booking's Change history.
  <Frame>
    ![Booking change history in the Distribution sidebar](/images/261016-distribution-sidebar-2.png "Booking change history in the Distribution sidebar")
  </Frame>

## Improvements

### The Periods page is easier to use

> For properties that manage peak season, holiday, and weekend periods

We refined the whole process of creating periods and linking them to packages in Settings \> Periods.

**Pick the method that fits the period you want to create**

The Create period screen now has three methods: Holidays, Date range, and Recurring.

<Frame>
  ![Create period](/images/create-period.gif "Create period")
</Frame>

- **Holidays**: Pick national holidays from the list and turn them into periods right away. You can create Holiday only, just the day before, or both the day before and the holiday. You can merge the day before and the holiday into one period or create them separately. Holidays you already created are marked, so you don't create duplicates.
- **Date range**: Set Start and End to create a period that applies once. (Example: summer peak season)
- **Recurring**: Create a period that repeats, such as every year or every week. You can combine months, dates, and days of the week.

---

**Create weekday and weekend periods at once**

<Frame>
  ![Select days](/images/select-days.gif "Select days")
</Frame>

If your weekday and weekend rates differ, you can split a period by day of the week and create several at once. 'Weekdays / Fri / Sat' or 'Weekdays / Weekend' are each created separately, so you can set a different rate for each.

---

**Set Adjustment values in advance**

<Frame>
  ![Period rate adjustment](/images/261016-period-rate-multiplier-1.png "Period rate adjustment")
</Frame>

When you create a period, you can also set an Adjustment as an amount or a percent. When you link the period to a package, that value fills in as the default selling rate. You can set it separately for each currency, so you can apply the Adjustment only to packages sold in a specific currency.

<Info>
  Creating a period alone doesn't change selling rates. The package sells at a different rate during that time only after you link the period to it. Periods that aren't linked yet show 'No linked packages'.
</Info>

---

**Set the packages to link to a period on one screen**

<Frame>
  ![Set package links](/images/connect-pkg-1.gif "Set package links")
</Frame>

Open 'Set package links' right from the period list to choose the packages to link to that period and their Adjustment values together. Packages are grouped by room type, and each package shows its Rate range, so you know right away the range to adjust within. Select all and Deselect all are also available.

---

**Delete periods that need cleanup at once**

<Frame>
  ![Delete period](/images/261016-delete-period.png "Delete period")
</Frame>

- **Delete past periods**: Clean up periods that have already ended, all at once.
- **Delete unlinked periods**: Find periods not linked to any package and delete them together.
