### A dropped channel connection shows at the top of the screen

> For properties running several connected channels

When a channel connection drops, a disconnected badge appears at the top of the screen. If the dropped channel belongs to the property you are running, a dialog appears too, so you know immediately.

<Frame>
  ![Disconnected channel](/images/260515-Disconnected-Channel.png)
</Frame>

**More detail**

- The dialog appears only when the dropped channel belongs to the property you are running, and it keeps appearing until the channel reconnects.
- With several channels down it reads **"\[channel name\] and N others disconnected"**.

<Warning>
  While a channel is disconnected, booking imports and sales sync stop. Stop-sells and rates do not reach the channel until it is back, so reconnect from the channel list right away.
</Warning>

### Large amounts read more clearly

> For properties selling high-priced rooms or long-stay packages

<Frame>
  ![High price display](/images/260515-High-Price.png)
</Frame>

Korean won amounts display better. The million-unit abbreviation is gone and hundreds of millions are supported, which fixes the large amounts that used to be hard to read at a glance.

---

### Values survive closing a settings dialog

Pressing ESC to close a settings dialog mid-edit used to wipe what you had entered. Fixed. Close a dialog by accident and your input is still there.

**More detail**

- The fix covers these dialogs:
  - Distribution \> View settings
  - Distribution \> Sell status view
  - Distribution \> Sell status and rate adjustment
  - Channels \> Unlinked package display setting

<Check>
  The shortcut that clears provisional changes in Distribution has moved from **ESC** to **Shift \+ ESC**.
</Check>

## Room types

### Max occupancy and room count accept larger numbers

> For properties with large room counts or high occupancy

<Frame>
  ![Room type maximums](/images/260515-Roomtype-Max-1.png)
</Frame>

The maximum occupancy and room count you can enter in room type settings have gone up. Properties that could not enter their real operating scale can now do so accurately.

## Channels

### The channel screen works on mobile

> For operators who check or manage channel settings while away

<Frame>
  ![Mobile channel screen](/images/260515-Mobile-Channel.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Mobile channel mapping](/images/260515-Mobile-Channel-Mapping.png)
</Frame>

The channel screen is available on mobile. Check connection states and manage the main settings from wherever you are.

**More detail**

- Channel packages that are not linked yet can be linked to VCMS packages from mobile.
- Commission and final channel rate can be set from mobile.

## Subscription

### Clearer subscription messaging

<Frame>
  ![Subscription messaging](/images/260515-Billing.png)
</Frame>

More of the situations you can hit during a subscription are explained, and the wording for each is better. When your subscription state changes, what to do next is easier to see.

<Frame>
  ![Cancelling a subscription](/images/260515-Billing-Cancel.png)
</Frame>

Also, before the free trial starts, you can cancel the subscription you requested yourself.

<Warning>
  To keep using the service after the trial ends, subscribe again. You can re-subscribe at any time after cancelling.
</Warning>

Radio buttons and tooltips now share one design and read more cleanly. Nothing functional changed. It is about a consistent screen across the product.
