> **Highlights**<br />"Find an accommodation by searching."

## New

### Find an accommodation by searching

> For owners running several accommodations

The accommodation picker in the top menu now has search. However many properties you run, you can find one by name and switch straight to it.

<Frame>
  ![Accommodation search](/images/260807-search-accommodation.png "Accommodation search")
</Frame>

<Info>
  With only one accommodation in use, search does not appear.
</Info>

## Improved

### Colours and styling tidied up

Colours and styling across the product have been cleaned up, so the screen reads better.

## Fixes

- (Mobile) Fixed the selectable date range in Distribution. Picking the last date on the calendar could jump backwards, and dates could display wrongly when switching from PC to mobile.
- Fixed the room type filter's "All" entry not refreshing when the language changed.
- Fixed an "Unexpected Application Error!" screen appearing in some situations.
