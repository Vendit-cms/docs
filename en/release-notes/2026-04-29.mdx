## Improved

### Yanolja hard block bookings now sync

Bookings on Yanolja hard block packages, which did not sync before, now come through the Vendit channel manager. Bookings on Yanolja packages tagged as hard block are visible in VCMS.

<Frame>
  ![Yanolja hard block](/images/Yanolja-Hardblock-1.png)
</Frame>

**More detail**

- Hard block channel packages are bought up in advance by the channel, so their inventory and rates cannot be adjusted.

<Warning>
  Hard block channel packages stay unlinked rather than mapped to a VCMS package. Mapping them would distort the sellable quantity.
</Warning>

---

### Agoda day use check-in and check-out times show in VCMS

The check-in and check-out times Agoda supplies for day use bookings now reach the Vendit channel manager, so you see the actual usage window on a day use booking.
