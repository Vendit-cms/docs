## Improvements

### See the current status of all your properties at a glance in the list

> For properties that run more than one Accommodation

Even with many properties, you can check the current status of each one right in the Accommodation list, without opening each property.

**Learn more**

- Each Accommodation card shows the Subscription status, whether a Payment method is registered, whether both selling and bookings are synced or only bookings, and the connected channels. Disconnected channels stand out.
- You can sort the list by the criteria you want. By default, the most recently created Accommodations appear first.
- Invitations you have not accepted yet stay pinned to the top of the list, so you do not miss them.
- If you run only one Accommodation, you go straight to Distribution after you sign in. If you have an invitation you have not accepted, the list screen opens first.

---

### Tell confirmed bookings apart right in the Sold list

> For properties that manage bookings by confirming them

In the Sold bookings list in Distribution, bookings you marked with 'Confirm' in Bookings now show a confirmed mark. You can see in the list which bookings you have already confirmed, and when you click the Confirm button, the change shows right away.

---

### Spot the scrollable area in the Sold list right away

Even when the screen is very small and the Sold list looks short, you can now tell right away that the area scrolls. The list always shows a scrollbar, and the tab area stays fixed at the top with a divider line.
