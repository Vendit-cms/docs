## Improvements

### See the current status of multiple properties at a glance in the list

> For properties that run multiple Accommodations

Even with many properties, you can check their current status right in Accommodations, without opening each one.

<Frame>
  ![Accommodations](/images/261029-accommodation-list-1.png "Accommodations")
</Frame>

**Learn more**

- See the Accommodation type, address, Created date, Subscription status, and whether a Payment method is registered right away.
- Sort the list by the criteria you want. By default, the list starts with the first Accommodation you created.
- Invitations you have not accepted yet stay pinned to the top of the list, so you won't miss them.
- If you have only one Active Accommodation, you go straight to Distribution after Sign in. If you have invitations you have not accepted, the list screen opens first.

---

### Tell confirmed bookings apart right away in the Sold list

> For properties that manage bookings by marking them as confirmed

<Frame>
  ![Confirmed booking](/images/261029-confirmed-booking.png "Confirmed booking")
</Frame>

In the Sold bookings list in Distribution, bookings you marked with 'Confirm' in Bookings now show a confirmed mark. You can see in the list which bookings you have already confirmed, and clicking the Confirm button takes effect immediately.

---

### Spot the scrollable area in the Sold list right away

Even when the screen shrinks and the Sold list looks short, you can now tell right away that the area scrolls.
