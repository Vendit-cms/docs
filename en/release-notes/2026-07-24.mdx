## New

### Subscribe directly in VCMS

> For properties on the free trial, or ready to subscribe

<Frame>
  ![Subscribing from the free trial](/images/260724-distribution-free-trial-subscribe-2.png "Subscribing from the free trial")
</Frame>

You can now subscribe to the service inside VCMS. Payment is taken as the free trial ends, so the service continues without a gap.

<Info>
  On the free trial, press the 'Free trial' button at the top to subscribe.

  <Frame>
    ![Free trial button](/images/260724-distribution-free-trial-subscribe-1-1.png "Free trial button")
  </Frame>
</Info>

<Info>
  Using booking sync only? Subscribe from the Subscription page.

  <Frame>
    ![Subscribing from the free plan](/images/260724-subscription-free-plan-subscribe-1-1.png "Subscribing from the free plan")
  </Frame>
</Info>

**More detail**

- The recurring payment date can be any day from the 1st to the 25th, and it can be changed.
- You can cancel your subscription at any time.

<Info>
  A scheduled cancellation ends at the next payment date. An immediate cancellation ends the subscription at once, under the refund terms. [Subscription](/en/billing/billing) covers cancellation, refunds, and what happens when a payment fails.
</Info>

### Pick the features you need, sized to your property

<Frame>
  ![Choosing a subscription](/images/260724-subscription-free-plan-subscribe-2-1.png "Choosing a subscription")
</Frame>

Subscribe to the configuration you need. Your subscription is based on the number of rooms, so as you grow you pay for the growth and nothing more.

**More detail**

- Inventory can be subscribed on its own. Rate goes with Inventory. (Rate alone is not available.)
- Add rooms or extra notification contacts and you pay for the addition.
- Annual payment comes with a discount.

### Flexible through the seasons

<Frame>
  ![Managing a paid subscription](/images/260724-subscription-paid-plan-manage-1-1.png "Managing a paid subscription")
</Frame>

The payment date, the items subscribed, and the configuration can all change mid-subscription.

**More detail**

- **Change payment date**: move it earlier and what you already paid is deducted from the next payment. Move it later and the extra days are billed first, with the regular subscription paid at the next payment date.
  <Frame>
    ![Changing the billing date](/images/260724-subscription-billing-date-change-1.png "Changing the billing date")
  </Frame>
- **Changing the configuration**: on Inventory and Rate together, you can drop back to Inventory alone.
  <Frame>
    ![Changing the configuration](/images/260724-subscription-paid-plan-manage-3-1.png "Changing the configuration")
  </Frame>

- **Scheduled cancellation**: you keep the service to the next payment date, and the recurring subscription ends there.
- **Immediate cancellation**: the service ends at once and you are refunded the amount less the period used. With no refund due, you keep the service to the next payment date.

<Warning>
  **Cancelling immediately has consequences**

  - Cancel Inventory immediately and channel sync stops at once, which can produce overbooking.
  - Cancel Rate immediately and channel sync stops at once, so rates edited in VCMS never reach the channels. Manage rates on the channels after cancelling.
  - Cancel extra notification contacts immediately and only the default contact keeps receiving notifications.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **On Naver, be careful when using Inventory alone**

  While the Naver channel is connected, stopping Rate can disturb Naver's own rate settings. Take particular care if your Naver room list has Today's Deal (a set discount applied if the room has not sold by 00:00) or holiday schedules registered.

  - Sync inventory and rates together and Today's Deal and the holiday schedules are deleted, replaced by the inventory and rates on the VCMS Distribution screen.
  - Sync inventory only and the deleted rate plan can leave packages unsold on holiday dates, or selling at a different rate registered earlier.
</Note>
