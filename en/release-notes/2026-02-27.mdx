## Improved

### Search bookings by sync date and payment date

> For properties managing booking data date by date

<Frame>
  ![Booking date filters](/images/image-27.png)
</Frame>

The date basis filter on the Bookings screen now offers sync date and payment date. Alongside check-in and check-out, you can search on when the booking actually happened, which makes settlement checks and point-in-time views easier.

**More detail**

- Payment date and time means when the synced booking actually sold on the channel.

---

### Bulk update selects room types, packages, and channel packages at once

> For properties changing many room types and packages together

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/image-29.png"
    alt="Bulk update selection"
    title="Bulk update selection"
    className="mx-auto"
    style={{ width:"51%" }}
  />
</Frame>

Bulk update in Distribution is better. Room types, packages, and channel packages can all be selected at once, so large changes go faster.

**More detail**

- Pick a date range in a month other than the current one and that month now shows immediately. It used to open on the current month, which made range selection awkward.

    ![Bulk update date range](/images/image-30.png)
  </Frame>

### Long accommodation names are readable in full

<Frame>
  ![Accommodation name tooltip](/images/image-28.png)
</Frame>

Accommodation names display better. A name truncated for length shows in full on hover, so with several properties you can be sure which one you are looking at.
