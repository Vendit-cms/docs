## New

### The Liveanywhere channel is connected

Liveanywhere, the short-term rental and month-long stay channel, is connected. Liveanywhere inventory and bookings can be managed from VCMS.

**More detail**

- How to connect: \[Channels\] - \[Available channels\], then click Liveanywhere.
- On the rental structure: unlike ordinary accommodation bookings, Liveanywhere works in **6-night or 30-night rental contracts**. What syncs is the contract, not an individual guest's booking.
- On sign-in: a Liveanywhere account signed up through social login cannot connect to VCMS. Use a phone number or email account.

<Warning>
  Liveanywhere rates **cannot be adjusted from VCMS**. Set and manage them on the channel.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **On the minimum-nights shortfall**

  Close individual dates and the dates caught between them can show as unbookable for **falling short of the minimum nights**.
