## New

### The Camperest channel is connected

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/Camperest-1.png"

    className="mx-auto"
    style={{ width:"46%" }}

</Frame>

Camperest, the campsite booking channel, is connected. Camperest inventory and bookings can be managed from VCMS.

**More detail**

- How to connect: \[Channels\] - \[Available channels\], then click Camperest.
- On sign-in: connecting to Camperest needs a campsite code. The [Camperest channel guide](https://docs.vcms.io/en/channels/camperest) shows where to find it.

<Warning>
  Camperest rates **cannot be adjusted from VCMS**. Set and manage them on the channel.
</Warning>

### The Banlife Stay channel is connected

Banlife Stay is connected. Its sales and bookings can be managed from VCMS.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/반려생활_stay.png"
    alt="Banlife Stay"
    title="Banlife Stay"
    className="mx-auto"
    style={{ width:"47%" }}
  />
</Frame>

**More detail**

- How to connect: \[Channels\] - \[Available channels\], then click Banlife Stay.

<Info>
  **How inventory works here**

  Banlife Stay has no concept of inventory quantity. Sales are managed purely by whether a room is open or closed. Inventory is set to 1, and bookings carry the sell rate only.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Banlife Stay rates **cannot be adjusted from VCMS**. Set and manage them on the channel.
</Warning>
