Camfit, the camping and glamping booking channel, is connected. Camfit inventory, bookings, and rates can be managed from VCMS.

**More detail**

- How to connect: \[Channels\] - \[Available channels\], then click Camfit
- On rates: Camfit allows up to 50 rates per room type. Past 50, only the first 50 counting from the nearest date are applied, and rates for later dates are not set.
- Multi-night rates take the same value as the single-night rate.
