## New

### Drag to copy rate adjustments in Distribution

> For properties that often adjust rates across many dates, such as peak season and weekends

<Frame>
  ![Bulk rate copy by drag](/images/260707-Inventory-Bulk-Update-1.png)
</Frame>

Drag a rate adjustment value across the Distribution screen and it copies to the whole range at once. Instead of setting each date, one drag applies the same rate to many.

**More detail**

- Rate adjustment copying also works at the 'All rooms' and 'Room type' levels. Copy at the higher level and it reaches the packages below.

<Tip>
  Show only Friday and Saturday (weekends) in View settings and one drag changes just the weekend dates on screen. It makes peak-season weekend rates quick to set.

  <Frame>
    ![Weekend-only drag](/images/260707-Inventory-Bulk-Update-2.png)
  </Frame>
</Tip>

## Improvements

### Distribution is faster and smoother

At accommodations with a great many packages and channel packages, moving between dates and expanding to the channel package level is much faster. Changing cell states, such as drag-copying a Stop sell across All rooms, is smoother too.

**More detail**

- Only what is on screen is loaded, so speed holds up however many packages and channel packages you have.
- Cell states such as rate adjustment and sell status are managed separately, so the screen stays stable when many cells change at once.
