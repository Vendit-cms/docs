## **Improved**

### The average package rate on the Distribution screen

> For properties like pensions, where a room type has a single package under it

<Frame>
  ![Inventory settings](/images/260630-Inventory-Settings.png)
</Frame>

Distribution now shows the average rate of the packages under a room type. Where a room type holds only one package, as at a pension, that average is the actual selling rate, so you read the real price straight off the screen.

**More detail**

- \[View settings\] - \[Room type rate display\] offers two modes: weighting and average package rate.
- Average package rate shows the average across the packages under the room type.
- Weighting shows the rate adjustment set on the room type.

<Info>
  The average is taken across the packages under a room type. Even with different rates per package, you get the room-type-level average at a glance, and where there is one package you see the exact selling price.
</Info>

### Select several booking statuses at once

> For properties wanting completed bookings and bookings assigned in the integration (VCLOUD) in one view

<Frame>
  ![Booking status filter](/images/260630-Booking-Status-1.png)
</Frame>

Two or more booking statuses can be selected together, gathering the bookings you want on one screen.

**More detail**

- Pick any two or more statuses and see them together.
- Select "Completed" and "Assigned" together and the bookings assigned in VCLOUD come along too.
