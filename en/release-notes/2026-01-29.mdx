## New

### Open/close channel packages share inventory too

> For properties also running open/close channels like Yanoljapension, Ddnayodotcom, and Airbnb

Inventory share has arrived. Channel packages that run on open/close rather than a room count, as Yanoljapension, Ddnayodotcom, and Airbnb do, can now be managed alongside channel packages that carry an availability number.

**More detail**

- As inventory falls, channel packages switch to Stop sell one at a time, starting from the bottom of the list. At zero, availability on every channel package goes to zero in turn.
- Yanoljapension, Ddnayodotcom, and Airbnb channels are capped at a quantity of 1.

<Info>
  Say a room type has 4 in inventory. Four packages stay open, and as inventory drops they close one at a time, starting from the bottom channel package. Inventory share means you are not switching sell status per channel by hand. Inventory syncs automatically.
</Info>
