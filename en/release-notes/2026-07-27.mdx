> **Highlights**<br />"Properties using inventory management alone no longer have the channel's unconfigured packages opened automatically."

## Fixes

### Unconfigured packages no longer open automatically under inventory-only management

> For properties using inventory management without rate management

At properties running inventory management without rate management, VCMS was opening the channel's unconfigured packages automatically. Fixed. Unconfigured packages now stay closed, and you decide on the channel when they go on sale and what they cost.

<Info>
  **What is an unconfigured package?**

  A package that has not been registered for sale on the channel yet. It is not sold on the guest-facing site. It shows up like this on the channel's own sales screen, for example the NOL Partner Center.
