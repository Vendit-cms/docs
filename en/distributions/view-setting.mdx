## **View settings**

![View settings](/images/en-view-settings.png)

<Info>
  Use View settings to build the Distribution screen you want.

  - **Date range**
    - Auto: the number of days shown follows your monitor resolution.
    - 15 days / 7 days: shows exactly that far ahead.
  - **Expand by**
    - Choose which level is expanded when the screen opens.
  - **Select days**
    - Show only the days you care about.
  - **Show**
    - Hide the rows you don't need and keep the ones you do.
</Info>
