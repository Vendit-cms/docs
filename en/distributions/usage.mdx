## **Changing sell status**

<iframe src="https://preview.vendit.co.kr/embed/cmd5r4c0o00egus0ibx0wlvum?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed&lang=English" width="100%" height="415" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

<Info>
  Sell status is quick to change.

  1. **Click the Stop sell area on the date you want and the sell status flips.**
     - Drag left or right to change several dates at once.
  2. **Press `Save` at the top to commit everything you changed.**
     - Saved changes are pushed to the channels together.
     - One exception: while sync is stopped, nothing reaches the channels. Check the sync state first.
</Info>

<Check>
  **When you change sell status on a parent item and a child item already has its own setting, you choose whether to keep that setting or overwrite it with the parent's.**
</Check>

## **Changing availability**

<iframe src="https://preview.vendit.co.kr/embed/cmd5r4c0o00egus0ibx0wlvum?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed&lang=English" width="100%" height="415" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

<Info>
  Availability is quick to change.

  1. **Click the availability area on the date you want and type how many rooms you want to sell on the channel.**
     - Drag left or right to change several dates at once.
     - Go past what you can actually sell (the number of rooms in the room type) and it is flagged as over-availability.
  2. **Press `Save` at the top to commit everything at once. Saved changes are pushed to the channels together.**
     - While sync is stopped, nothing reaches the channels. Check the sync state.
</Info>

## **Adjusting rates for every room at once**

<iframe src="https://preview.vendit.co.kr/embed/cmd5ud8pr010nus0iry8westh?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed&lang=English" width="100%" height="415" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

<Info>
  Rates across the board are quick to change.

  1. **Click the rate adjustment area on the date you want.**
  2. **Set the markup or discount you want and every room rate for that day moves together.**
     - By amount: adds to or subtracts from the package rate.
     - By percentage: raises or lowers the package rate by a set percentage.
       - Extra amount on top: add or subtract a further amount.
  3. **You see the final price before it applies.**
  4. **Press `Save` at the top to commit everything at once. Saved changes are pushed to the channels together.**
     - While sync is stopped, nothing reaches the channels. Check the sync state.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Adjusting rates for every room, or for one room type, in one go.** Every package moves except those on a locked rate or a fixed rate. Packages with a rate range only move within that range.
</Warning>

## Changing check-in/out times

<iframe src="https://preview.vendit.co.kr/embed/cmeh3bfh00006z20ifhcdg6yt?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed&lang=English" width="100%" height="550" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

<Info>
  The check-in/out times set per package and per weekday can be overridden on a specific date.

  1. **Click the check-in/out time on the date you want.**
     - Change every package together, or one at a time.
  2. **Press `Save` at the top to commit everything at once. Saved changes are pushed to the channels together.**
     - While sync is stopped, nothing reaches the channels. Check the sync state.
</Info>
